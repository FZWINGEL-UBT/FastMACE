    ASE front end¶

    calculator ¶

    FastMACECalculator and fm.calc: the ASE calculator, compiled tier (SPEC §7.4, §7.7 level 0).

    ASE calls a calculator once per step, so this front end gets the compiled tier at most (§8.2): a compiled model, exact edge counts, no CUDA graphs. Full speed needs FastMACE to own the MD loop (fm.md, the fastmace.ase integrators). The calculator builds nothing itself: at the first calculation, and again whenever the species or the atom count change, it makes a one-off evaluation plan through the minimal planner (MinimalPlanner.plan_evaluation: compiled, with stress, no workload) and gets that plan's MACEForceField from build_evaluation. Each plan's superset is its structure's species (owner decision D3), so new species get a fresh plan; the plans share one compiled-model cache. Evaluation plans compile with a dynamic edge dimension (EngineConfig.dynamic_edges, 2-T13), so relaxations, phonopy and NEB, whose edge counts change between calls, pay one compile.

    FastMACECalculator ¶

    Bases: Calculator

    FastMACECalculator(model, *, head=None, rewrites="auto", precision="float32", dispersion=None): energy, free_energy, forces and Voigt stress in ASE units; full 3D PBC only (§7.6).

    • model: an fm.load result or a label of the model table (a checkpoint path or URL needs fm.load or fm.calc with sha256=/trusted=, since the trust decision needs one); head selects a head of a label's checkpoint, and must match a loaded model's head;
    • rewrites: "auto" or a RewriteConfig; precision: the force precision, "float32" or "float64". Energies are accumulated in float64 (§7.3);
    • dispersion: None, "d3bj" or an fm.D3, as fm.ForceField takes it (MACE plus D3, SPEC §11.2; 3b-T04). dispersion is then the complete record. With D3 there are no per-atom energies (D3's energy is not split by atom here).

    energies (per atom) is listed only for models whose energy terms are all atom-resolved; PolarMACE's k-space electrostatics are not (§7.3). Non-periodic or mixed-PBC atoms raise PlanRefused (rule 1); gas-phase molecules need MACE's MACECalculator (§4.3).

    plan is the current evaluation plan (None before the first calculation) and notes what the calculator recorded, the compatibility-tier note first; the note is printed once.

    calc ¶

    calc(
    model: Any, *, head: str | None = None, **options: Any
) -> FastMACECalculator

    atoms.calc = fm.calc("mh1", head="matpes_r2scan"): a FastMACECalculator for model (label, path or URL, loaded as fm.load does); prints the compatibility-tier note once.

    options: fm.load's sha256, trusted, download, charge and spin_multiplicity, and the calculator's rewrites, precision and dispersion.

    md ¶

    ASE MD scripts on the graph engine: fastmace.ase.Langevin, LangevinBAOAB and the phase 4 classes (SPEC §7.7 level 1).

    An ASE script is ported by changing the import and adding model=. Each class takes the argument names and units of its ASE 3.27 class (SUPPORTED) and refuses the rest with the reason (REFUSED); any other argument is a TypeError naming it. The FastMACE additions are keyword-only (ADDITIONS). The shim never reinterprets an ASE argument: friction, the pressures and the time constants are in ASE units, and the conversion to the public integrator record is the only arithmetic (the record's to_config converts back, so a value may differ by one ulp).

    The centre of mass (owner decision D2, conductor's answer to 4-T10): a class whose ASE original takes fixcm (Langevin, NVTBerendsen, NPTBerendsen) passes it through and follows decision 37, as fastmace.ase.Langevin does (LangevinBAOAB keeps its phase 1b mapping). A class whose ASE original has no fixcm (VelocityVerlet, Bussi, NoseHooverChainNVT, IsotropicMTKNPT, MTKNPT) removes no centre of mass, as ASE, and counts ASE's degrees of freedom: its keyword-only dof defaults to "ase"; dof="auto" (or "fastmace") is FastMACE's convention instead, the total momentum removed and 3N - 3 counted (fixcm="auto": with FixAtoms no momentum is removed and 3(N - n_fixed) counted, 4-T04). FixAtoms runs where owner decision 44 allows it (NVE, CSVR, NVT Berendsen, Langevin; with fixcm=False for the classes that take ASE's fixcm, whose default True is refused with it); rule 1 refuses it elsewhere at the first run, with the reason.

    Nothing here builds an engine: the classes build a fm.Simulation at the first run or step, which plans, runs the optional gate (owner decision D1: check="gate") and builds through the minimal planner (SPEC §5). The setup report is printed then.

    run(n) advances the Simulation in chunks that end where an observer is due (ASE's attach rule: every interval steps from step 0, or once at step |interval| for interval <= 0). Before the observers of a step the committed state is written into atoms with a single-point results cache as atoms.calc; after them, changed positions or momenta changed species, masses, initial charges, positions or momenta become one event through Simulation.update (SPEC §8.5; species in the superset), and any other change raises PlanRefused with its reason. The shortest observer interval is the workload's host_callbacks_every (rule 9). trajectory= and logfile= keep their ASE meaning (one ASE Trajectory, ASE's MDLogger format) and are written from the Simulation's asynchronous snapshots; every other file goes to output.

    Langevin ¶

    Bases: _Dynamics

    ASE 3.27's ase.md.langevin.Langevin on FastMACE's graph engine (Langevin, SPEC §10.2).

    Langevin(atoms, timestep, temperature=None, friction=None, fixcm=True, *, temperature_K=None, trajectory=None, logfile=None, loginterval=1, append_trajectory=False, model=None, force_field=None, head=None, seed=None, output=None, check="skip", dispersion=None, fixcm_projection="auto"): ASE's arguments and units (timestep and 1/friction in ASE time units, temperature in eV, deprecated as in ASE); rng, a non-default comm/communicator and master are refused. The additions are those of fm.md, and fixcm_projection (fm.integrators.Langevin's; decision 37). With fixcm, "auto" resolves to the mass-weighted projection: the log then reads its frames with ASE's FixCom, so its temperature counts 3N - 3 (the trajectory frames stay unconstrained). fixcm_projection="ase" is ASE's Langevin exactly (its noise stream, 3N). FixAtoms runs with fixcm=False (ASE's default fixcm=True is refused with it, owner decision 44); the log and the trajectory frames carry the constraint, so temperatures count 3(N - n_fixed).

    optimize ¶

    ASE relaxation scripts on the graph engine: fastmace.ase.LBFGS and FIRE (SPEC §7.7; decision 48).

    An ASE script is ported by changing the import and adding model=. Each class takes the argument names, units and defaults of its ASE 3.27 class (SUPPORTED) and refuses the rest with the reason (REFUSED): restart other than None (a relaxation writes no restart files, conductor's choice 5), LBFGS's use_line_search=True, FIRE's deprecated maxmove and position_reset_callback, and a non-default comm/master. The FastMACE additions are keyword-only (ADDITIONS).

    atoms is an Atoms (fixed cell) or ase.filters.FrechetCellFilter(atoms, ...) (variable cell, read into fm.optimize.FrechetCell); every other filter (UnitCellFilter, ExpCellFilter, StrainFilter, ...) is refused with the reason (decision 48).

    Nothing here builds an engine: the classes build a fm.Simulation(optimizer=...) at the first run, irun or step, and run(fmax, steps) is Simulation.relax in chunks that end where an observer or a trajectory frame is due (ASE's attach rule). logfile is ASE's optimiser log (ASE's Log, "-" by default as ASE's), one line per step from the relaxation's per-step energies and max forces; trajectory= is one ASE Trajectory with a frame every loginterval steps. Before the observers of a step the relaxed state is written into atoms (positions and cell) with a single-point results cache as atoms.calc. An observer that changes atoms raises: the optimiser's history would no longer describe the structure (conductor's answer to 4-T10).
