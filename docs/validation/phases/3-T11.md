# 3-T11: the move-energy gate and the MC energy precision

Plan task 3-T11 (`plans/phase-3.md`), 22 October 2026. Spec: §12.3 "move-energy differences",
§10.4 "Energy precision" and "Accuracy prerequisite", §9.3 (the fifth dimension), §9.4 step 2,
decision 35 (owner D5). Acceptance item 3 of phase 3.

## What exists now

- **The sample moves** (`planner/selfcheck.py`, `sample_moves`). The gate runs before any engine
  exists and before `run(mc=…)` declares its records, so it samples whatever a record could declare:
  - **swaps:** 2 for each unordered pair of superset species. A pair with a species absent from the
    structure is sampled on a base where another atom was first transmuted into the absent species,
    if transmutations are declared. Otherwise it is recorded as "not sampleable", because that pair
    can never occur (conductor answer 6);
  - **transmutations:** one random atom of each present species, to every other superset species,
    absent ones included;
  - **displacements:** 4 random atoms moved by exactly `Workload.max_displacement_A` (answer 5), the
    largest trial any `Displace` may make.
  The generator is seeded from the run's seed with its own salt, so the samples are the same in every
  process and never touch an MC stream.
- **The references** (`move_references`). The float64 reference energy of every sample configuration
  comes before the engine is built, on the gate's ladder: the plan's device in a child process, then
  the CPU in float64. There is no reduced cell, because the sample moves belong to the production
  structure. A reference that cannot run is recorded as `unavailable`; it never raises.
- **The rule** (`move_verdict`): every sample's `|ΔE_prod − ΔE_ref| ≤ 0.01 k_B T_min`, with T_min =
  `Workload.min_mc_temperature_K`. A non-finite value fails before any maximum is taken. There is no
  ratio term.
- **The production half** (`move_gate`). ΔE_prod comes from the engine's `MCEvaluator`, through the
  captured energy-only graph in graph mode. Each configuration is a T03 transaction from the committed
  state that its evaluation rejects, so the state is restored bit for bit. Displacements re-compact the
  neighbour list inside the transaction. A trial that would need a rebuild is not sampled, and that is
  recorded. With rule 11's skin, which includes 2δ, it does not happen right after a build.
- **The precision** (`PrecisionConfig.mc_energy`; `planner/fixed.py`, `planner/minimal.py`):
  - **no MC moves:** the force precision (decision code `mc-energy-no-mc`);
  - **an MC plan with "auto":** the force precision, pending the gate (`mc-energy-gate-pending`). If
    the gate fails, the MC energies move to float64 while MD forces keep their precision, and the
    check is repeated. A reference that is `unavailable` gives float64 (decision 35);
  - **an explicit request:** kept and gated (`mc-energy-requested`). If it fails, `GateFailed` reports
    the measured error (answer 4). float32 MC energies under float64 forces are refused
    (`mc-energy-below-force-precision`).
- **The record.**
  - `GateRecord.move_energy` holds the move-energy part: status, requested and chosen precision,
    threshold, T_min, superset, samples per type, what is not sampleable, the worst error per move
    type in eV and in k_B T_min, each precision tried, every row, the references and the timings.
  - `GateRecord.mc_energy_precision` is the chosen precision.
  - `GateRecord.status` stays the energy, force and stress gate's: `skipped` under `check="skip"`
    (answer 3).
- **The fallback** (`planner/fallback.py`):
  - `DIMENSIONS` puts `mc_energy` first;
  - `candidate(plan, {"mc_energy": "float64"})` is a revision that re-plans nothing;
  - `mc_energy_revision(plan, record)` records the gate's choice as an `mc_energy` revision with its
    `TriedCandidate`;
  - `move_energy_only(failure)` detects a failure of the move-energy part alone;
  - `Fallback(..., moves=)` tries float64 MC energies first when only the move-energy part failed
    under "auto". It then re-validates the move energies of every diagnosis run and candidate
    (`activate(..., moves=)`).
- **Build.** `MinimalPlanner.build` accepts a structure whose species lie inside a larger superset
  (rule 8). Before this, it required equality, so an MC plan with an absent superset species could not
  be built.
- **References and the default dtype.** `evaluate_references` puts torch's process-wide default
  dtype back after MACE's loader changed it (the 3-T07 note).
- **Child results.** A reference child's result holds only the roles it evaluated: the move
  references need the float64 reference alone. The GPU test found that `_read_result` assumed both.

The names are in `docs/design/contracts-3.md` ("The move-energy gate and the MC energy precision")
and pinned in `tests/cpu/test_contracts_3.py`.

- **`Simulation`** (second editor of `simulation.py` after T15, answer 1):
  - `move_references` runs after the gate's references, before the build, whatever `check` is;
  - `move_gate` runs after the production gate. The plan is revised by `mc_energy_revision`, and its
    `state.plan_revision` follows;
  - a failure goes to `Fallback(..., moves=)`;
  - the MC driver is attached with an evaluator in `PrecisionConfig.mc_energy`: the gate's float64
    copy, or a new one where the reference was unavailable.
- **`report.py`:** the `mc ΔE:` line gives the MC energy precision, the move-energy gate's status, the
  worst error per move type in k_B T_min against the limit, and the precisions tried. The report
  record has `move_energy_status` and `mc_energy_precision`. `Simulation` now passes a `skipped` gate
  record that carries a move-energy part.

## Acceptance item 3: float32 ΔE errors against float64

**Setup.**
- **Machine:** local PC, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti, WSL, `~/environments/mace_cueq_oeq`.
- **Plan:** MH-1 (`~/models/mace-mh-1.model`) heads `matpes_r2scan` and `omol` (all-electron E0), hybrid
  float32 graph plan (`MinimalPlanner`, MC workload over the superset {Na, Nb, O, Cl}, rule 11).
- **Structures:** 3×3×3 from the 1a-T18 baseline export.
- **Commits:** FastMACE 57d788f (MH-1 `matpes_r2scan` NaCl), cc57ebc (`matpes_r2scan` NbCl5 and
  NaNbO3), e5b988f (`omol`). The code paths measured are the same in all three; the tool records no
  commit for these runs because WSL git cannot read the worktree (fixed in 0c69f97: it records
  `FASTMACE_SOURCE_COMMIT`).
- **Tool:** `tools/validation/move_energy_precision.py`.

**Protocol.**
- One engine per structure: NVT at 300 K for 2000 steps of 1 fs, then 1500 K for 2000 more. After
  each, a canonical point (the neighbour list rebuilt at the snapshot) and then the measurement.
- **Samples** (`sample_moves`, more than the gate's defaults): 4 swaps per superset pair, 2 atoms per
  present species transmuted to every other superset species, 8 displacements of 0.15 Å.
- **ΔE_ref:** the gate's float64 reference, in its GPU child process.
- **ΔE_prod:** the engine's MC evaluator: float32 (its own force field, captured energy-only graph),
  and float64 (the float64 copy of the same plan, hybrid backend).
- **Units:** the worst |ΔE_prod − ΔE_ref| per move type, in k_B·T_min with T_min = 250 K (the
  annealing protocol's), so the gate's limit is **0.01**; µeV and the sample count in brackets.
- Raw rows: `results/3-T11-rtx5060ti-20261022/<model>-<structure>/metadata.json`.

**The float64 copy** (the "float64" side), against the same reference:
- MH-1 `matpes_r2scan`: at most 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ k_B T_min (2.5 × 10⁻¹² eV);
- MH-1 `omol`: at most 2.1 × 10⁻⁶ k_B T_min (4.5 × 10⁻⁸ eV, NbCl5).

Its rows are in the result files.

**Reading.**
- Every float32 ΔE error is below the gate's 0.01 k_B T_min at T_min = 250 K.
- The worst is 0.0035 (76 µeV), on NaNbO3 swaps at 1500 K. That is a margin of about 3. It shrinks
  in proportion at lower T_min: the gate would fail float32 for these cases below about 90 K.
- The errors grow with cell size and with disorder (1500 K snapshots), not with the E0 convention.
  MH-1 `omol` with all-electron E0, whose float32 totals are spaced about 2 eV apart (§10.4), errs as
  little as `matpes_r2scan`, because the energy is accumulated in float64 and the E0 sum is exact.
- Swaps carry the largest errors. They change two atoms' environments, and an absent-species pair
  goes through a transmuted base.

**What "auto" picks.** For these models and structures at T_min ≥ 250 K: the force precision (float32).
The gate measures it per run.

**Cost of the measurement.** Per structure: build 23–47 s, MD 15–62 s per temperature, the
references 23–87 s for 63–65 configurations, the float32 side 5–13 s, the float64 side 14–29 s.

- 1024 channels;

The MD and the float32 production path are cheap. The cost is the float64 reference (see the open
findings).

## Open findings

1. **The gate's references ran at charge 0 for a charged model** (fixed here,
   `reference/calculators.py`). A gate copy is rebuilt from numbers and positions, so MACE's
   calculator did not see the model's total charge and spin.
   - It affected the move-energy part and, since 1b, the energy, force and stress gate of any model
     loaded with a non-default charge or spin.
   - Found on the tiny charge-input model with total charge 1: 0.27 eV ΔE differences in float64.
   - The MH-1 and OMOL-0 runs here and in earlier tasks load without a charge (`None`, MACE's
     defaults on both sides), so they were not affected.
2. **A rejected transaction leaves scratch the registry does not hold.** The force field's
   `_species_index` keeps the trial's species. The gate refreshes the species- and charge-derived
   buffers after its trials, so a gated engine equals a fresh construction (3-T09's tests found it).
   T03's `Transaction.reject` and T09's rejected attempts restore the registered buffers only. They
   are correct today, because every refresh rewrites the scratch before reading it, but
   `events.check_against` reports the difference after a rejection. This is for T03 or T09 to decide.
3. **A head without its own label (`mh-1:omol`) lost its head on a reload by label** (fixed here,
   `model/labels.py`). That covered the plan's execution model and the gate's reference children.
   Before the fix, MH-1's `omol`, `spice_wB97M` and `oc20_usemppbe` heads could not be planned
   through `fm.load("mh1", head=...)`.
4. **OMOL-0 XL's float64 references are too slow for the gate on this hardware.** The move-energy
   part needs one float64 forward per sample configuration. For OMOL-0 XL that is minutes each on
   the RTX 5060 Ti, so its gate takes far longer than §12.3's "seconds to a minute". Options:
   - an energy-only reference evaluation for the move-energy part (no forces or stress: no
     backward pass);
   - fewer default samples for large models;
   - accept `unavailable` there, with float64 MC energies (decision 35).
   The first is the general one; it also halves the gate for every model.
5. **Not wired through the subprocess runner's report.** The runner's child runs `Simulation`, so
   the gate runs there. Whether the parent's report shows the "mc ΔE" line has not been checked.
