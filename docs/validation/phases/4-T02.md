# 4-T02: NVE, CSVR and Berendsen

Plan task 4-T02 (`plans/phase-4.md`), 23 October 2026. New schemes on 4-T01's registry:
`fastmace/integrators/nve.py` (ASE `VelocityVerlet`), `csvr.py` (ASE 3.27 `Bussi`) and
`berendsen.py` (`NVTBerendsen`, `NPTBerendsen`, `Inhomogeneous_NPTBerendsen`), with their records in
`fastmace/integrators/schemes/`.

## Deviation from ASE: NPT Berendsen makes one force call per step

ASE's NPT Berendsen step scales the cell, and `set_cell(scale_atoms=True)` invalidates the
calculator cache, so ASE evaluates the forces twice per step: the first kick uses forces at the scaled
geometry. FastMACE makes one force call per step; the first kick uses the previous step's forces
(conductor, 23 October 2026: Berendsen is non-canonical, and twice the cost for exact equality is not
worth it). The tests compare it to ASE's class reordered the same way (`Reordered*` in the tests) and
record its distance from ASE's own step. NVT Berendsen and CSVR keep ASE's step exactly.

## CPU (tiny MACE, float64, `tests/cpu/test_nve_csvr_berendsen.py`)

NaCl 2 x 2 x 1 conventional cells (32 atoms), 300 K, zero momentum, 200 steps of 1 fs, `dof="ase"`,
against the ASE class on the same calculator. Acceptance: positions and velocities within 1e-12.

| Scheme | max position difference (Å) |
| --- | --- |
| NVE vs `VelocityVerlet` | 2.7e-15 |
| CSVR vs `Bussi` (noise batch 7) | 5.3e-15 |
| NPT Berendsen vs reordered `NPTBerendsen` | 2.5e-14 |
| Inhomogeneous vs reordered `Inhomogeneous_NPTBerendsen` (mask 1, 0, 1) | 1.1e-13 |

NVT Berendsen and the clamped scaling (lambda 0.9 and 1.1) pass the same 1e-12 limit. Against ASE's
own two-call step, NPT Berendsen differs by 9.6e-6 (isotropic) and 2.4e-6 (inhomogeneous) in the
relative volume and by 2.4e-4 and 2.8e-4 Å in the positions, while the volume moved by 7.6 % and 5.1 %;
the test bounds are 1e-4 and 2e-3 Å. Also tested: restart round trip and interrupted against
uninterrupted, bitwise, for every scheme; buffer policies and fixed addresses; CSVR's noise stream
(ASE's per-step `standard_normal`, `standard_gamma`); D2's degrees of freedom; refusals; a run through
the planner and `Simulation` with thermo output for each scheme (NVE's `target_K` column is NaN: it
has no target).

## GPU (`tests/gpu/test_nve_csvr_berendsen_gpu.py`)

Machine: local PC (WSL2), NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti; environment `mace_cueq_latest` (torch
2.14.1+cu130, mace 0.3.16, cuequivariance-torch 0.12.0, ASE 3.27.0). FastMACE `fastmace/4-T02` at
cf39fb5 (the integrator code of the final head); no research-engine baseline is involved.

- **CUDA graphs (fast tier):** each scheme's half steps captured with a capturable harmonic force,
  50 replays bitwise equal to the eager steps: 5 passed.
- **ASE equivalence (conductor's choice 2, acceptance tier):** MH-1 `matpes_r2scan`, NaCl 3 x 3 x 3,
  plain cuEq float64, 4,000 steps of 0.5 fs at 300 K from a 300 K zero-momentum start, `dof="ase"`.
  The integrators run eagerly with the state on the GPU against the ASE class on a second
  `MACECalculator` (NPT Berendsen against the reordered class). The engine's run of the new schemes
  is 4-T10's and 4-T13's. Limits per 500-step block: |ΔT| < 1e-6 K, |ΔPE| < 1e-7 meV/atom.

| Scheme | 4,000 steps | max block |ΔT| (K) | max block |ΔPE| (meV/atom) | final max |Δx| (Å) | growth (1/fs) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NVE | pass | 6.8e-13 | 8.9e-13 | 4.8e-14 | 1.7e-3 |
| CSVR | pass | 4.5e-13 | 8.9e-13 | 6.5e-14 | 1.8e-3 |
| NVT Berendsen | pass | 4.0e-13 | 1.8e-12 | 8.0e-14 | 1.6e-3 |
| NPT Berendsen | pass | 9.7e-13 | 8.9e-13 | 2.1e-13 | 1.3e-3 |
| Inhomogeneous NPT Berendsen | pass | 2.4e-12 | 1.8e-12 | 1.4e-12 | 1.6e-3 |

Every scheme meets choice 2's limits over the full 4,000 steps, so the shortened horizon is not needed
(checkpoint 2). The growth rate is the log-slope of the position difference between steps 100 and
4,000; at about 1.6e-3 per fs the 1e-13 Å rounding of plain cuEq float64 would reach 1e-6 K only after
some 10 ps. Each run took 16–17 minutes of GPU (two force calls per step on the host-driven path). JSON:
`results/4-T02-ase-equivalence-20261023/`.
