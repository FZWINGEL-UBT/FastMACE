# 4-T04: `FixAtoms`

Plan task 4-T04 (`plans/phase-4.md`), 23 October 2026, owner decisions 44 (D1 (a)) and 45 (degrees of
freedom 3(N − n_fixed), no projection). Design: `docs/design/physics.md`, "`FixAtoms` in the integrators".

## What changed

- **Rule 1:** `langevin`, `nve`, `csvr` and `berendsen` declare `fix_atoms`. Refused, with the reason:
  `fixcm=True` with `FixAtoms` (`fix-atoms-with-fixcm`), any variable cell (`fix-atoms-variable-cell` for
  NPT Berendsen; BAOAB, NHC and MTK by their records), MC with constraints (decision 10), any other ASE
  constraint. LBFGS and FIRE took `FixAtoms` at fixed cell already (4-T05, 4-T06).
- **Integrators**, on a mask path that runs only with fixed atoms (without them every step is unchanged):
  Langevin resets the fixed atoms after the drift and zeroes their velocities after the second kick; NVE and
  CSVR zero the fixed velocities after the first kick and recompute every velocity from the displacement, as
  ASE's `VelocityVerlet` does with any constraint; NVT Berendsen zeroes the fixed velocities after each kick.
  The fused Triton stage's drift kernel has a `FIXED` variant.
- **`fixcm="auto"`** in `fm.integrators.Langevin`, `NVE`, `CSVR`, `Berendsen`: off with `FixAtoms`.
- **State, output, resume:** fixed atoms start at rest; temperatures count 3(N − n_fixed);
  `Simulation.resume` rebuilds `FixAtoms` from the restart's `fixed_atoms`.
- **`fastmace.ase`:** `FixAtoms` accepted with `fixcm=False` (ASE's default `fixcm=True` is refused: pass
  `fixcm=False`); an observer that changes the constraints is refused; frames carry the constraint and the
  raw forces; the observer cache's `get_forces()` applies it.
- **Reference:** the D3 part of `ReferenceCalculator` and `reference.d3.torch_dftd3` read every atom's forces
  (the constraint would have zeroed the fixed rows, and the gate compares all of them).

**Pipelined against checked: rounding, not bitwise.** The first run of the NVE and CSVR trajectories
(locally, `4-T04-fix-atoms-trajectories-nve-csvr-first-run.json`) asserted that the pipelined and the checked
run end bitwise equal; they differed in the last bits. (1) Why: the pipelined engine rebuilds the neighbour
list early, when an atom has moved `pipeline_rebuild_fraction` of the half skin (the speculative flag), the
checked engine only at the hard limit. So the two rebuild at different steps (NVE 126 against 103, CSVR 179
against 146) from different positions; the candidate lists, their padding buckets and the order of the edge
sums differ, and the forces differ in the last bits from the first early rebuild on. The fixed atoms are not
involved: they stay bitwise in place in every run. (2) Bound: positions within 1e-9 Å and energies within
1e-9 eV after 600 steps, three orders of magnitude above what main shows without `FixAtoms` (below) and far
below any physical difference; chosen so that a real divergence (a wrong mask or a lost kick moves atoms by
1e-3 Å or more within a few steps) fails it. (3) Main without `FixAtoms` is not bitwise either
(`tools/modal/4_t04_pairs.py` on main 0e17745, L4 / Modal, `pairs-main-0e17745/pairs.json`):

| Integrator (main 0e17745, no constraint) | bitwise | max \|Δx\| (Å) | max \|Δp\| | ΔE (eV) | rebuilds pipelined / checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NVE | no | 3.9e-14 | 3.7e-13 | 4.5e-13 | 130 / 107 |
| CSVR | no | 1.9e-13 | 1.7e-12 | 4.5e-13 | 185 / 149 |
| Langevin (fused stage on in both) | no | 8.0e-13 | 7.9e-12 | 0 | 166 / 137 |

So the expectation, not the library, was wrong; OPT3-T03's trajectory test compares the fused stage on and
off within one pipeline mode, which is why Langevin's pairs there are bitwise.
