# 3b-T06: the D3 cost on the production ramp

23 October 2026. Plan task 3b-T06 ([phase-3b.md](../../plans/phase-3b.md)); SPEC §11.2 "Cost target",
§9.3 rule 11, decision 42 (a miss is recorded and the phase continues).

**Result: the 10 % target is missed.**
- **Dense phase (NVT at the crystal's volume, 1000 K): D3 adds 24 %.** That is 4.95 ms on a
  20.68 ms step, against a 2.07 ms budget.
- **The D1 route at 0 GPa (NPT, the anneal ramp): about 15 % amortised over the 270 ps.** That
  figure is low only because the chloride vaporises: the box grows from 55,900 Å³ to 2.3 × 10⁸ Å³
  by 276 K, so the D3 pairs, and with them its cost, almost vanish.
- **The cost is the three passes, not the builds.** The passes take about 4.2 ms per step at
  1.8 million pairs. Builds add 0.73 ms per step at 1000 K and 0.37 ms per step while heating.
- **Rule 11's D3 numbers stay as T02 set them** (n = 50, floor 1.0 Å). At 1000 K they give
  2.04–2.07 Å, which is the measured optimum; the cost is flat within ±0.1 ms per step from 1.5 to
  2.5 Å.

## Setup

- **Machine and environment:** the local PC (Fabi-PC, WSL2) with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti,
  driver 610.88. Environment `~/environments/mace_cueq_oeq` (torch 2.14.1+cu130), run through
  `tools/ci/local_gpu.py run`.
- **Model and backend:** MH-1 `matpes_r2scan` (`mace-mh-1.model`, sha256 a522eb7f…) on the hybrid
  backend, float32 forces through the minimal planner, with the D3 reductions in float64.
- **Structure:** NbCl5 3×3×3, 1944 atoms, 55,910 Å³ at the start.
- **Integration:** 1 fs, seed 20261022. NPT means hydrostatic BAOAB at 0 GPa with the library's
  barostat defaults; NVT means ASE Langevin.
- **Commits:** FastMACE 3176bf9 (branch `fastmace/3b-T06` on main 667bedc). No research-engine
  baseline is involved.
- **Tool:** `tools/bench/d3_cost.py`, with the per-run launcher in the conductor's notes folder
  (`3b-T06-run.sh`). Results are in `results/3b-T06-rtx5060ti-20261023/`
  (`scan-npt`, `ab-npt`, `ab-nvt`: `.json` and `.log`).
- **Route:** the anneal profile's heating, 0 → 1000 K in 20 ps. Then 8 ps windows of its 3 K/ps
  cooling at 1000 K, 600 K and 300 K, with 100 K/ps transits between them. The thermostat target is
  set before every step from each engine's own step count, as the protocol's `TargetFill` does.
- **A/B:** a D3-off and a D3-on engine in one process, timed in 1000-step blocks that alternate
  ABBA. Every rebuild, MACE's and D3's, falls inside the clock. Two NPT engines share a process
  without trouble, so nothing is timed across processes.
- **Quiet PC:** each run held the GPU reservation. A pytest process was seen before 5 of 128
  blocks, all in the heating segment. The overheads without those blocks equal the overheads with
  them (median 37.2 % against 34.5 % while heating; the other segments are untouched). Both runs
  are valid: no other process used the GPU, and nothing recompiled while timed.

- **NPT at 0 GPa vaporises NbCl5, with D3 and without it.** The cell volume, D3 on (D3 off in
  brackets):

  | Point on the route | Volume, Å³ |
  | --- | --- |
  | Start | 55,900 (55,900) |
  | 1000 K | 1.5 × 10⁵ (1.9 × 10⁶) |
  | 600 K | 2.1 × 10⁶ (8.4 × 10⁸) |
  | 276 K | 2.3 × 10⁸ (5.8 × 10¹¹) |

  At 0 GPa a gas has no equilibrium volume, and the barostat keeps expanding the box. This is the
  outcome D1's alternative (b) anticipated, 0.1 GPa "to keep the molecular chloride condensed at
  1000 K". It concerns 3b-T07's protocol run and the owner, not D3.
- **The NPT overhead compares two different systems.** The D3-off box expands faster, so its MACE
  step gets cheaper sooner (20.3 → 15.5 ms). For that reason, D3's cost on a condensed system is
  the NVT number.
- **Amortised over the D1 ramp:** the 20 ps of heating at 36.8 %, then 250 ps of cooling. The
  cooling is interpolated linearly in temperature between the windows: 1000 → 600 K at about 15.8 %,
  600 → 300 K at about 9.8 %, 300 → 250 K at about 8.6 %. Together that is about 15 %. The
  production rate cools more slowly than these transits, so the box has longer to expand and the
  true figure is lower still. 3b-T07's full protocol run records the D3 build count and time for the
  cross-check.

- **The skin barely matters in the vapour.** The trajectory's 1000 K frame is already at 3.6 × 10⁵
  pairs. There the amortised cost is 1.82–1.99 ms for every skin, and at 600 K and 300 K it is
  0.86–1.17 ms. The passes have a floor of about 0.8 ms even at 6000 pairs.
- **Dense phase.** Take the NVT run's measurements at 2.04 Å: a pass of about 4.2 ms, a D3 build
  every 45 steps at 1000 K, and 33 ms per build as the engine counts it (this includes waiting for
  the force call that reads the list). Scale the passes with the pair count, which grows as
  (21.17 + s)³, and the rebuild rate with the heating segment's counts above. The amortised D3 cost
  is then:

  | Skin Å | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 4.0 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | ms/step | 5.5 | 5.1 | 5.0 | 5.1 | 5.3 | 5.7 |

  The minimum is near 2 Å and flat from 1.5 to 2.5 Å.
- **Rule 11 as merged** (`neighbors/adaptive.py`: `D3_TARGET_REBUILD_STEPS = 50`,
  `D3_FLOOR_SKIN_A = 1.0`, the barostat strain term) gives 2.04 Å for NVT and 2.07 Å for NPT at a
  1000 K maximum. That is the optimum, so **no change** (conductor's answer Q1). The strain term
  added 0.03 Å at 1000 K: the library's barostat (P_tau = 20 T_tau) is slow, and the melt–quench
  volume swing builds up over hundreds of rebuild intervals, not within one.
- The measured rebuild interval at 1000 K and 2.04 Å was 45 steps, close to the rule's n = 50. The
  rule's ballistic displacement bound is not too conservative in the dense liquid.

## Why it misses, and what would help (follow-up, not this task)

- **The step is cheaper than SPEC §11.2 assumed.** §11.2's budget of 2.9 ms comes from a 29 ms
  hybrid step. Here the MH-1 hybrid step without D3 is 20.4–20.7 ms (dense), so the 10 % budget is
  2.07 ms.
- **The three passes alone take 4.2 ms** over about 1.8 million stored pairs. That is roughly
  2.3 ns per pair across the three passes, or about 3 × 1.8 M pair reads per step, with a fixed
  floor of about 0.8 ms. The SPEC's estimate of 150–200 MB per step at bandwidth was optimistic for
  this kernel.
- **Builds** cost 33 ms each in the engine, every 45 steps at 1000 K. That is 0.73 ms per step, 15 %
  of D3's cost.
- **Directions,** to be judged by the conductor and the owner:
  - fuse passes 2 and 3 for pairs whose dE/dCN is complete;
  - store r² or the pair vector per pair instead of recomputing it from the shifts;
  - a CN-only pass over the short `cnthr` cutoff;
  - a smaller cutoff for the C8 term;
  - a lower-precision pair loop.

  Each changes the kernel of 3b-T03 and needs its own gate. Under decision 42 they are a follow-up.
