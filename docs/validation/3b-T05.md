Status (23 October 2026):

- D3 in the NPT path is done: exact against ASE on the CPU and on the GPU.
- The independent comparison ran. It passes on V, the V fluctuation, P and the 600 K drift, but misses

  seeds per engine is the conductor's decision.

Plan: [phase-3b.md](../../plans/phase-3b.md), task 3b-T05. Spec: §10.1 (targets as device scalars filled
before a step), §11.2 (correctness criterion).

## The pressure setter (SPEC §10.1)

- `LangevinBAOAB.set_pressure(pressure_GPa)` and `set_external_stress(stress_GPa)` take GPa, ASE sign:
  a scalar, or six Voigt components for the full cell, with `IntegratorConfig.external_stress_GPa`'s
  validation. `set_externalstress(value)` takes ASE units, the constructor keyword's forms.
- Each is a `copy_` into the device tensor `externalstress`, which the captured graphs read: no
  recapture, no quiesce. The arithmetic is the config's, so a target filled to P equals one configured
  with P bit for bit.
- Fixed-cell integrators (`Langevin`) refuse both setters (`NotImplementedError`).
- **Half-step lag** (approved by the conductor): the new target enters at the next step's cell-force
  update, so that step's first barostat half kick still uses the previous target. The same happens in
  ASE when `externalstress` is replaced between steps.
- **Restart:** the integrator record stores the current target exactly, in ASE units
  (`"externalstress"`). `restart.restore_targets` fills temperature and pressure on resume, and
  `Simulation._resume` uses it. Files from before 3b-T05 keep the configured target.

### The double buffer

**Bug found and fixed:** stage B must not read the filled target directly. A pipelined engine
validates step n inside step n+1's call, after the host has filled step n+1's target. When it redoes
B(n) (rebuild or edge-count overflow), B(n) saw target(n+1). Under a pressure ramp, pipelined and
checked engines then differed by up to 1e-4 Å.

**The fix:** BAOAB keeps a second tensor, `externalstress_step`:
- stage A copies the filled target into it;
- stage B's `_update_force_eps` (and the registry's refresh of the cell force) reads it;
- a resume fills both tensors.

The temperature fill never had this problem, because `sqrt_kT` is read only in stage A. Both tensors
are registered (derived; [contracts-1b](../design/contracts-1b.md) table).

### Tests (CPU, float64)

`tests/cpu/test_pressure_setter.py`, 25 tests:
- **ASE match:** a 0 → 2 GPa ramp reproduces ASE `LangevinBAOAB` whose `externalstress` is replaced
  between steps, hydrostatic and full cell, within 1e-9.
- **The fill:** in place (same tensor, same address); Voigt components; a filled target equals a
  configured one bit for bit.
- **Pipelined:** pipelined replayed engines equal eager ones under a ramp, with a count redo forced
  after a fill. With `externalstress_step` aliased to the filled target (the bug), both cell modes
  fail.
- **Restart:** the record holds the current target; an interrupted ramp resumes bit for bit, in
  checked and pipelined modes and both cell modes, with a target at the restart that differs from the
  configured one. Old files keep the configured target.
- **Refusals:** fixed-cell integrators, non-finite, boolean or malformed targets, and Voigt components
  for a hydrostatic cell.

CPU suite (22 October 2026, `fastmace_cpu`, commit 15f0561, `-n 6`, `-m "not slow and not
acceptance"`): 3162 passed, 242 skipped. Ruff clean.

## D3 in the NPT path and the independent comparison

### D3 in BAOAB's barostat

`_update_force_eps` reads only `state.stress`, and 3b-T04's `DispersedForceField` returns MACE's stress plus
D3's (−virial/V). So BAOAB needed no change for D3. The tests check the path end to end.

**CPU** (`tests/cpu/test_npt_d3.py`: tiny MH-1-class model, NaCl, D3 on its torch tier, float64):
- **Cell force:** `force_eps` equals BAOAB's formula with the engine's stress to 1e-12, at the start and
  after 15 steps, in both cell modes. That stress equals MACE alone plus torch-dftd to 1e-8 eV/Å³.
  With MACE's stress alone the cell force would differ by more than 1e-4.
- **After an MC event:** an accepted species swap refreshes `force_eps` with the new total stress
  (§8.5 step 6; the refresh takes the state's velocities, as before D3).
- **Trajectory:** 40 hydrostatic NPT steps at 0.5 GPa follow ASE `LangevinBAOAB` with
  `SumCalculator(MACE's own calculator, torch-dftd)` and the same seed, to 1e-8 Å in cell and positions.

**GPU** (`tests/gpu/test_npt_d3_gpu.py`; the PC's RTX 5060 Ti, `mace_cueq_latest`; FastMACE 7219e1f +
the report line):
- **Setup:** tiny MH-1-class model on NaCl 2×2×2 (64 atoms), a float64 plan, D3 on its Triton passes
  with a 0.05 Å D3 skin, captured graphs. 100 NPT steps at 600 K and 0.5 GPa, against ASE
  `LangevinBAOAB` + MACE + torch-dftd (float64), same seed.
- **Result:** checked and pipelined engines both agree with ASE to 0.0 Å in the cell, 2.7e-14 Å in
  positions and 5.1e-14 in momenta, with 40 and 44 D3 list builds during the run. The cell changed by
  0.4 %.
- Numbers: [`gpu-test/3b-T05-npt-d3-vs-ase.json`](../../results/3b-T05-npt-d3-comparison/gpu-test/3b-T05-npt-d3-vs-ase.json).

### The independent comparison (conductor's choice 7; SPEC §11.2)

D3 in the NPT path waited for 3b-T04. The settings agreed with the conductor:
- NbCl5 1×1×3 (216 atoms), 300 K and 600 K, 0 GPa, 1 fs, hydrostatic;
- P_tau 2 ps as a test setting, barostat mass factor 1, friction 0.02 per ASE time unit;
- 3 seeds per engine;
- the ASE side with float32 cuEq MACE and float64 torch-dftd.

The pass rule is 3b-T10's runbook, item 2: |ΔX| ≤ 3 combined SE.

### Prepared: the ASE side and the analysis

[`tools/validation/npt_d3_comparison.py`](../../tools/validation/npt_d3_comparison.py) has two commands:
- **`run-ase`:** ASE `LangevinBAOAB` driven by MACE's own `MACECalculator` (cuEq, float32,
  `matpes_r2scan`) plus `torch_dftd3_calculator(fm.D3(functional="r2scan"), float64)`, summed by
  `SumCalculator`. No FastMACE code is in its force path or integrator.
- **`analyse`:** the pass rule.
  - Blocks of at least 2 τ of the slowest of V, P and PE, with the means' SE pooled over the seeds.
  - The V fluctuation's SE is a jackknife over the same blocks.
  - Each run reports its V drift, so a non-stationary 600 K run is recorded as such.
  - `tests/cpu/test_npt_d3_comparison.py` checks the statistics on AR(1) series with known answers.

**Local smoke** (RTX 5060 Ti, `mace_cueq_latest`, 22 October 2026): 500 steps at 600 K, 216 atoms,
73.4 ms per step after the first 10 % (86.3 including start-up). One run of 5 + 20 ps (25,000 steps) is
then about 31 min locally.

**On Modal** ([`tools/modal/3b_t05.sh`](../../tools/modal/3b_t05.sh), one run per container): the image
gains `torch-dftd==0.5.1`; its `ase` and `pymatgen` were already pinned.

### The result

The analysis's blocks are 2 τ of the slowest observable. At 300 K that is PE: τ(PE) ≈ 49 samples (about
the thermostat's T_tau), against τ(V) ≈ 24, so blocks are 99 samples, 60 per engine. The first version
sized blocks from τ(V) alone and underestimated the PE error (4.3 SE; corrected here).

- At 600 K, V grows from about 32 to 49 Å³/atom in the 20 ps of production: the crystal is
  evaporating. The 15 October study's 5 ps at 0.5 fs only looked bounded.

- The stationary rule's numbers at 600 K are reported in `comparison.json` but not judged.

**Verdict under the pre-declared rule: not passed.** Two of the judged observables miss, both narrowly:
300 K PE at 3.45 SE and 600 K window 1 at 3.01 SE. The rest pass. The rule's one pre-declared extension, 3
more seeds per engine, is the next step, and it is the conductor's call.

### What the PE difference is not

- **Not the energy function.** [`tools/validation/npt_d3_energy_offset.py`](../../tools/validation/npt_d3_energy_offset.py)
  evaluates the ASE runs' final frames with FastMACE's production `evaluate`, with the ASE side's
  calculator, and with that calculator in float64 (all plus D3).
  - **All six frames:** FastMACE is within 0.0004–0.0008 meV/atom of float64, and the ASE side's float32
    within 0.012–0.014.
  - **Sign:** on the same frame FastMACE is 0.011–0.014 meV/atom *higher* than the ASE side, the opposite
    sign to the sampled −0.77.
  - Numbers: [`energy-offset.json`](../../results/3b-T05-npt-d3-comparison/energy-offset.json); RTX 5060
    Ti, `mace_cueq_oeq`, FastMACE 2599774 plus the probe.
- **Not the float32 energy sum of the ASE side** (1b-T03's effect): that is in the 0.015 above.
- **Not the integrator, the `Simulation` front end or the tool.** From identical velocities and noise
  streams, `fm.Simulation` (the tool's `fastmace_side`, float64, tiny model, D3) and the tool's
  `run_ase` agree for 60 steps to 1e-13 in T and 2e-15 eV/atom in PE (a scratch probe). The CPU and GPU
  tests above agree with ASE as well.

The PE difference goes with a 5.5 K difference in the mean kinetic temperature (3.7 SE), and PE changes
by about 0.13 meV/atom per K. In configuration space too, the FastMACE runs sample a slightly colder
crystal: lower PE, and 0.05 Å³/atom less volume. Neither side sits at the 300 K target, though: ASE is
3.2 K above (3.2 SE), FastMACE 2.3 K below (2.1 SE).

The production FastMACE path differs from every exact check above in its hybrid float32 force field and
in its early rebuilds beside the force call. The checks cover the integrator, the D3 term with list
rebuilds, and the energy function. Whether the 3.5 SE is chance (with 3 seeds per side) or a small
systematic difference in the production path is open. The extension's seeds, and a FastMACE run on a
float64 plain-cuEq plan at 300 K (cheap, 3 seeds about 15 min), would separate the two.
