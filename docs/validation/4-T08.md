# 4-T08: Annealing-benchmark minima

Plan task 4-T08 ([plans/phase-4.md](../../plans/phase-4.md)); SPEC decision 46 (the owner's D3 (a)), §10.3,
§16 row 4. 23 October 2026.

**Result: fails decision 46, on one frame of 210.** NaTaCl6 frame 12 has |dE| 0.188 meV/atom against the
0.025 limit. The other 209 frames are within 0.0074 meV/atom. All 420 relaxations converged. Every crystal has
at least 20 of 21 frames with the same structure. The failure is recorded until the owner decides, and the
criterion is unchanged. The frame's probe is [below](#natacl6-frame-12).

## What was compared

- **Frames:** the research engine's 210 `captured.traj` quench frames: NaCl and the nine production crystals,
  21 each, 3x3x3, from ResearchModule `Experiments/Langevin_Thermostat/results/anneal-*-rtx5060ti-20261010/`.
  These are untracked files in that checkout; its `.gitignore` excludes `results/**/*.traj`.
  - `tools/validation/anneal_minima.py fetch` copied them (read only) into
    `~/fastmace-data/anneal-minima/`, with the 10 October BFGS minima (`quenched.traj`, `quench.csv`) and the
    13 October ASE LBFGS minima (`lbfgs.traj`, `comparison.csv`).
  - `verify` checks the 51 files against `tools/validation/anneal_minima.sha256` before every evaluation.
- **GPU side:** `Simulation(atoms, ff, optimizer=fm.optimize.LBFGS())`, `sim.relax(fmax=0.01, steps=5000)`.
  - ASE 3.27 LBFGS defaults: memory 100, maxstep 0.2 A, damping 1, alpha 70. Fixed cell.
  - Graph mode, skin "auto" (rule 11's optimiser skin, 0.6 A).
  - One engine per crystal relaxes the 21 frames in turn through `update(cell=, positions=)`.
- **ASE side:** ASE 3.27's `LBFGS` with the same arguments on `fm.calc` (`FastMACECalculator`, the compiled
  tier). Same model, head and precision, so only the optimiser differs. `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2`.
- **Model:** MH-1 `matpes_r2scan`, hybrid float32, `~/models/mace-mh-1.model`.
- **Tools:** `tools/bench/optimizer_frames.py` (4-T05), extended with `--frames all`, `--skins auto` and
  `--resume`. One process per side and crystal; the sides' order alternates per crystal. Then
  `tools/validation/anneal_minima.py evaluate`.
- **Pass rule (decision 46):**
  - all 210 converge, on both sides;
  - per frame, |E_GPU - E_ASE| <= 0.025 meV/atom;
  - in at least 20 of 21 frames per crystal, every atom is within 0.1 A (minimum image) of the ASE minimum.

## NaTaCl6 frame 12

MD frame 6167, cooling, 875 K target. Both sides converged (fmax 0.0086 and 0.0099 eV/A).

| Minimum | E (eV/atom) | Steps | Distance to GPU minimum, A |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| GPU LBFGS (L4) | −12.762264 | 310 | — |
| ASE LBFGS on `fm.calc` (L4) | −12.762453 | 470 | 2.60 (RMS 0.11) |
| Research engine, BFGS, 10 Oct | −12.762286 | 310 | 0.0045 |
| Research engine, LBFGS, 13 Oct | −12.762283 | 296 | 0.119 |

- The GPU minimum is the research engine's minimum for this frame: within 0.0045 A of its BFGS minimum. The
  13 October BFGS–LBFGS comparison already marked this frame as one of its two outliers (0.12 A).
- ASE on `fm.calc` went on to a different structure 0.19 meV/atom lower.

**Probe** (`tools/validation/anneal_frame_probe.py`, commit 3066709, Modal L4). Four fresh processes, each
the frame alone: GPU and ASE LBFGS, float32 and float64, with the positions after every step. Each also
re-relaxed the other side's minimum from job C. Results in
[results/4-T08-20261023/probe-NaTaCl6-12/](../../results/4-T08-20261023/probe-NaTaCl6-12/).

1. **Two minima, both stationary.**
   - Job C's two minima are 2.60 A apart at most (RMS 0.11 A), and 0.188 meV/atom apart.
   - Each optimiser, started from the other side's minimum, takes 0 steps: both already meet fmax 0.01
     eV/A (0.0099 and 0.0086). On this tolerance they are two minima, not one minimum and a point
     on a slope.
2. **ASE's lower minimum does not reproduce.** In a fresh process, ASE LBFGS on `fm.calc` (float32) takes
   310 steps to the GPU's minimum, within 0.0004 A, not 470 steps to the lower one.
3. **Where the runs part.**
   - The probe's float32 GPU and ASE trajectories differ by more than 1e-6 A from step 2 and by more than
     1e-4 A from step 53. They never reach 0.01 A, and the minima end 0.006 A and 0.0003 meV/atom apart.
   - Job C's 470-step trajectory was not recorded step by step, and it did not happen again. So its first
     divergence cannot be placed.
4. **Float64:** GPU LBFGS and ASE LBFGS agree within 1.6e-12 A at every one of 310 steps. Both reach the
   GPU's minimum (float32 and float64 minima within 0.006 A).

**Replay of job C's ASE side:** frames 0–12 of NaTaCl6 in one process, as job C ran them, twice in separate
processes on an L4 (results in
[results/4-T08-20261023/replay-NaTaCl6-ase/](../../results/4-T08-20261023/replay-NaTaCl6-ase/)).
- Frame 12 took 311 and 310 steps, both to the GPU's minimum.
- The earlier frames repeat job C's minima within 0.05 ueV/atom, except where the step count changed from
  run to run:
  - frame 6: 211, 220 and 211 steps;
  - frame 10: 357, 357 and 367 steps;
  - frame 11: 300, 295 and 300 steps.
- So ASE on `fm.calc` in float32 is not bitwise repeatable from run to run on these frames. Job C's 470-step
  path was one outcome of that rounding, not an effect of the frames before it in the process.

**Reading:** the frame sits where two minima 0.19 meV/atom apart are both reachable. Which one a float32
LBFGS finds depends on rounding at the 1e-6 A level. GPU LBFGS is ASE's LBFGS: identical in float64. In float32, ASE
reached the GPU's minimum in 3 of its 4 runs of this frame (job C's is the exception), and GPU LBFGS in both
of its runs. This
is evidence for the owner's decision. The criterion and the recorded failure stand until then.
