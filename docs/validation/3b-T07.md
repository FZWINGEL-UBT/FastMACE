23 October 2026. Branch `fastmace/3b-T07`. Plan task 3b-T07, acceptance item 4 of phase 3b (SPEC §16
row 3b; owner decision D1 = SPEC §18 decision 40).

**Result:**
- The protocol is data (`fastmace run npt_anneal_NbCl5`). Its smoke variant ran end to end on the local
  RTX 5060 Ti: the fixed-cell relaxation with D3, NPT with D3 along the anneal schedule, and the quench of
  21 frames, each at its own NPT cell. Every stage and the D3 counters are recorded.
- The full D1 run reached step 74,380 of 270,000, then stopped at a non-finite energy, as SPEC §9.6

- **Outcome, recorded and not judged (decisions 6 and 40):** at 0 GPa, NbCl5 3×3×3 with MH-1
  `matpes_r2scan` + D3-BJ vaporises near the 1000 K plateau. The vapour has no finite volume at zero
  external pressure, so the barostat expands the cell without bound on the cooling ramp. In the smoke run
  it went to 447 Å³/atom; in the full run the expansion was exponential, to 7.0e12 Å³/atom (a cell side of
  about 2.4e5 Å) at the stop.
- The pressures stay physical throughout: they fall to the ideal-gas scale kT/V, about 5e-13 GPa at the
  end. NbCl5 boils at about 520 K at 1 bar.
- Whether to repeat the run at D1's alternative (b), 0.1 GPa, is the owner's call.

## The protocol

`fastmace/protocols/data/npt_anneal_NbCl5.toml`:

| Item | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Structure | NbCl5, C2/m, the R2SCAN benchmark cell (`NbCl5_R2SCAN.cif`, source in `data/SOURCES.md`), 3×3×3: 1944 atoms |
| Model | MH-1, head `matpes_r2scan`; hybrid cuEq/OEq, float32 |
| Dispersion | `"d3bj"`: D3(BJ), the `r2scan` set (s6 1, s8 0.7898, a1 0.4948, a2 5.7308), 40 bohr, no ATM; float64 reductions; non-stock (§12.1) |
| Relaxation | ASE LBFGS at fixed cell to fmax 0.01 eV/Å, on MACE plus D3 (`fm.calc`, float32) |
| Ensemble | hydrostatic NPT at 0 GPa, `LangevinBAOAB` with the library's barostat defaults (P_tau = 20 T_tau, mass factor 1) |
| Schedule | the `anneal` profile at 1 fs: 0 → 1000 K in 20 ps, then 3 K/ps to 250 K (270,000 steps) |
| Output | thermo rows (with volume and pressure) every 10 steps, frames every 100 steps, a restart every 10,000 steps |
| Quench | the anneal profile's: 2 frames per 100 K bin from 500 K on each ramp, and the final frame; LBFGS to fmax 0.01 eV/Å, max 5000 steps, MACE plus D3, each frame at its own NPT cell with the cell fixed |

- **Frames every 100 fs, not main.py's 10 fs (decision 36), on purpose (conductor, 22 October):** a full
  run then writes 2,701 frames of 1944 atoms, a few hundred MB instead of about 4 GB. A 100 K quench bin
  on the cooling ramp still spans about 330 frames.
- `npt_anneal_NbCl5_short.toml` is the same protocol with a schedule a tenth as long: 0 → 1000 K in
  2 ps, then 30 K/ps to 250 K (27,000 steps).

## Code

- `Profile` gets `ensemble` (`"NVT"` or `"NPT"`), `pressure_GPa` (NPT's constant target, 0 GPa by
  default) and `dispersion` (`"none"` or `"d3bj"`), read from profile files too. `md_options()` gives
  `fm.md`'s arguments. Pressure schedules are refused: D1 needs a constant 0 GPa. Wiring 3b-T05's setter
  into the protocol's run loop, with its restart semantics, is a follow-up.
- The relaxation and the quench use the run's dispersion (`FastMACECalculator(..., dispersion=)`), so
  they relax on the same MACE plus D3 surface as the MD. `protocol.json` names the D3 record.
- `protocol.json` records per piece the counters of `runner.PIECE_COUNTERS` and their sums over the run
  (`counters`). Each piece builds a new engine and force field, so their counters restart at zero.
  - `neighbour_rebuilds`: the MACE list's rebuilds (`Engine.info()`);
  - `d3_builds`, `d3_rebuilds`, `d3_build_s`: the D3 term's counters (the force field's `info()["d3"]`).
  - 3b-T06 reads them for its full-ramp cost cross-check.
- `quench.csv` gets `volume_A3_atom`. `quench.json`'s settings say `cell = "frame"`: each frame is
  relaxed at its own cell, with the cell fixed.

- The crystal starts to expand during heating, near 900 K. After the 1000 K turn it vaporises, and the
  volume then grows by about 14× every 4,600 steps. The barostat integrates the log of the volume, so a
  small positive gas pressure against 0 GPa gives exponential growth.
- At the stop the cell side is about 2.4e5 Å. There, float32 coordinates resolve only about 0.02 Å. The
  NaN is consistent with that, but it has not been traced to a kernel.
- In the short run the same happens on a compressed schedule. The volume is 46 Å³/atom at the 1000 K turn
  and 447 Å³/atom at 250 K; the vapour does not recondense in 25 ps of cooling.

## Left over

- **A complete full run:** D1 at 0 GPa cannot finish once the crystal vaporises. A run at 0.1 GPa (D1's
  alternative (b)) or a lower peak (c) is the owner's call.
- **A volume guard:** the protocol could stop cleanly, with a named reason, once the volume passes a
  limit, instead of running into a non-finite energy. This was not asked for in 3b-T07.
- **Pressure schedules in protocols** (3b-T05's setter): refused for now, a follow-up.
- **The NaN's mechanism** at a 2.4e5 Å cell is not traced. An evaluation of the last frame on the CPU
  probably exhausted WSL's memory: a cell list over that box needs about 1e8–1e9 bins. The run is not
  repeated.
