# 4-T03b: the fused Nose-Hoover chain stage

Follow-up to [4-T03](4-T03.md), agreed with the conductor on 23 October 2026. The faithful port's
unrolled chain loops were about 600 scalar kernels per MD step: NHC cost 370 µs, MTK 1.0–1.5 ms.

## What changed

- **One chain algorithm for both stages.** ASE recomputes the chain force `sum(p**2 / m)` after
  each momentum scaling and scales the momenta three times per half step. The new chain half
  reduces the force once and carries it through each scaling s as `F s**2`, then scales the momenta
  once by the product of the s. The algebra is the same; the results differ from ASE in rounding
  only. Every constant (the sub-step deltas over the chain masses) is a host float in a float64
  table.
- **`exp_and_exprel`** (MTK's full cell) is a Horner Taylor series with scaling and squaring:
  `exprel(2B) = exprel(B)(exp(B) + I)/2` and `exp(2B) = exp(B)²`. The 3 × 3 products are
  `times_3x3`'s element-wise sums, so a Triton program can reproduce them.
- **`fastmace/kernels/triton/nose_hoover_chain.py`** runs a chain half and the matrix functions as
  one single-program kernel each. Both kernels use the PyTorch stage's arithmetic in its order:
  float64, coefficients loaded from float64 tensors, FMA contraction off. This is OPT3-T03's
  pattern.
- **`fused=None`** (the integrators' constructor keyword) picks the kernels on CUDA when Triton is
  installed, and the PyTorch stage otherwise. `fused=True` refuses without CUDA.

## Step time

`tools/nhc_fused_timing.py`; one process per part, alternating blocks, median per step. RTX 5060 Ti,
NaCl 3×3×3 (216 atoms). JSON files in `results/4-T03b/` (not in git).

**Stages A + B** (CUDA graphs, harmonic stand-in field, `fixcm` on, 10 blocks of 1,000 steps):

| Scheme | fused (µs) | PyTorch (µs) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NHC | 77 | 374 |
| MTK, hydrostatic | 407 | 983 |
| MTK, full | 458 | 1,459 |
| Langevin (yardstick) | 78 | — |

NHC now costs what Langevin does. MTK's remaining time is in its PyTorch kicks and drifts (the
ideal-gas stress, determinants, the exprel scalars), not in the chains.

**MH-1 end to end** (`fm.Simulation`, the planner's default plan, 600 K, 1 fs, 10 blocks of 300
steps; MTK full cell at 0 GPa):

| Scheme | fused (ms) | PyTorch (ms) | change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NHC | 6.21 | 6.50 | −4.4 % |
| MTK, full | 7.00 | 8.07 | −13 % |

The blocks do not overlap (NHC: fused 6.17–6.38 ms, PyTorch 6.46–6.65 ms), so the gain is larger
than the 3 % cross-process noise floor and was measured within one process. Commits: FastMACE
`fastmace/4-T03b` (on 4-T03 `356d605`, main `352b33c`).

## Decision

Adopted, under the conductor's rule "adopt if it passes". The plan's wording changes from "device
scalar operations, unrolled at capture" to "fused" (`plans/phase-4.md`, answers section).
