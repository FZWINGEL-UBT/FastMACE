# 3-T05: adaptive skin and headroom (rule 11)

Phase 3 (`plans/phase-3.md`, task 3-T05; conductor answers of 22 October 2026). SPEC §9.3 rule 11, §10.4
(displacement skin), §8.1 (buckets). Date: 22 October 2026.

## The rule (`fastmace/neighbors/adaptive.py`)

The skin list stays valid while no atom has moved more than skin/2 since its build, so the skin sets the
rebuild rate. Rule 11 uses no tables:

- **The fastest atom.** sigma = sqrt(k_B T_max / m_min) is one velocity component's deviation for the
  lightest mass. The speed of an atom in units of sigma is Maxwell-distributed. z_N is the speed that about
  one of N atoms exceeds: N · [erfc(z/√2) + √(2/π) z e^(−z²/2)] = 1. For example, z_216 = 3.61 and
  z_1944 = 4.20.
- **Ballistic per step.** d = z_N · sigma · dt. Over a rebuild interval (tens of fs), the ions keep their
  velocity.
- **Skin:** max(0.3 Å, 2 · n* · d), rounded up to 0.01 Å, with n* = `TARGET_REBUILD_STEPS` = 16 steps
  per rebuild. Displacement moves add 2 · δ_max (`Workload.max_displacement_A`).
- **Pair-buffer headroom:** max(1.15, 1 + 3 · (skin/2) / (r_c + skin)). Over one interval, the shell of
  candidates within skin/2 of r_c + skin turns over.
- **Re-capture budget:** 2 · ceil((headroom − 1) / edge_bucket_fraction) + 1. These are the padded edge
  buckets that the count can visit inside the capacity, above and below the first count.

**Check against data.** The model predicts a rebuild every 24 steps for NaCl at 300 K and 0.5 fs with the
0.3 Å skin; 1a-noise-floor measured about 25. At 300 K and 0.5 fs, NaCl, NbCl5 and NaNbO3 stay on the
0.3 Å floor.

**Where it applies** (conductor answer 3-T05 1–2):
- **MC moves or declared species changes:** `"auto"` takes the rule's values.
- **MD-only workloads:** `"auto"` keeps 0.3 Å and 1.15, so default plans do not change. The decisions
  `workload.max_temperature_K` and `NeighbourConfig.skin` record what the rule would give.

**Other resolutions:**
- Explicit values are kept: rule 11 is a preference.
- Evaluation plans and `FixedPlanner` plans have no workload and keep 0.3 Å.
- The lightest mass is the lightest of the structure's masses and the superset species' standard masses.

The fused integrator reads `neighbours.skin` when it is constructed (`fused_integrator.py:207-209`), so it
follows the planned skin. A CPU test pins this.

A larger skin does not change the edges within the cutoff of a receiver-sorted list, nor their order. The
CPU test `test_a_larger_skin_keeps_the_sorted_edges_within_the_cutoff` compares 0.3 Å with 0.9 Å. So the
skin changes the rebuild rate, not the forces.
