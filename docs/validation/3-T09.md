# 3-T09: MC driver, swaps and transmutations

Plan task 3-T09 (`plans/phase-3.md`), 22 October 2026. Spec: §10.4 (all but displacements), §8.5
"Random numbers per outcome", §7.6 (MC blocks), §12.2 "Mutation events", "MC exact enumeration".

## What exists now

- **`fastmace/mc/streams.py`.** Counter-based Philox4x32-10 streams (Random123's known answers), numbers by
  (seed, offset), the fixed counts per attempt, the swap and transmutation decoding and `BlockStreams`
  (the device side of a block). The layout is in `docs/design/contracts-3.md`, "MC streams and the driver".
- **`fastmace/mc/moves.py`.** `Attempt`: propose → apply → refresh → energy → accept mask → restore as
  fixed-shape device operations, one energy stage per edge size (`MCEvaluator.stage`), so the driver
  captures it with `GraphEngine.captured`. `metropolis` (`ln u < −(ΔE − Δμ)/kT`) and `charge_inputs`.
- **`fastmace/mc/momenta.py`.** `exchange`, `rescale`, `redraw` (per move) and `redraw_all` (per block,
  zero total momentum).
- **`fastmace/mc/driver.py`.** `MCDriver(moves, *, seed, config=None)`:
  - `attach(engine, *, evaluator=None)`, `due(step)`, `run_block(engine, step)`, `last_block`, `counters`,
    `offsets`, `state_dict`/`load_state_dict` (the restart's `mc` record, also put into `state.mc`), `info`;
  - one host read per block: counters, attempt indices and the overflow word, with the block's device
    time (CUDA events);
  - the first capture of an attempt runs without effect: the capture's warm-ups restore the engine's
    registered state but not the driver's tensors, so the driver snapshots both around it;
  - after a block with an acceptance: `redraw_all` where chosen, the mass refresh, the total charge as a
    host value, `canonicalise(reevaluate=True)`, then the force-derived caches (T03's resume).
- **`planner.minimal.check_mc_moves`.** Rule 2, the `fixcm` refusals of `rescale` and `redraw`, and the
  charge/spin parity (only for models or heads with charge or spin inputs; otherwise the decision
  "parity: not applicable").
- **`io.restart.resume_engine`.** Restores a recorded total charge through `set_total_charge` (3-T08's open
  point).
- **`tools/bench/mc_cost.py`.** The swap-attempt rows (second editor after T07).

## Deviations

- **`MinimalPlanner._check_atoms`** required the structure's species to *equal* the plan's superset, so a
  `Simulation` with a superset species absent at setup (NaCl with superset Na, Cl, K) could not be
  built, although T02's rule 8 plans it. It now requires the species to be *within* the superset. No test
  pinned the equality.
- **Graph keys** are `("mc.<move>", driver id, index, precision, edges)`. Without the driver's id, a second
  driver on the same engine replayed the first driver's captured attempt (found by the cost tool).
- **`build_attempt`** takes a keyword-only `fixcm` (the default treatment of a transmutation depends on
  it); `MCDriver.attach` takes a keyword-only `evaluator`. `test_contracts_3.py` pins both.
- **`state.total_charge`** stays the host record of a total-charge change, as T03's `set_charges` keeps it:
  the planner leaves it `None` at construction, also for charged models. After a transmutation that moves
  the total charge it holds the new value, so the restart records it.

## Cost: one swap attempt as a fraction of one MD step (acceptance item 2, swap part)

- **Setup:**
  - local PC, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti, WSL;
  - `~/environments/mace_cueq_oeq` (in-process OEq runtime);
  - MH-1 `mh1-r2scan` (`~/models/mace-mh-1.model`);
  - plan backend hybrid float32, default MD plan (`MinimalPlanner`), 1500 K;
  - structures from the 1a-T18 baseline export, 3×3×3 (NaCl 216 atoms, NbCl5 1944 atoms);
  - float64 MC energies from T07's float64 copy (copy backend hybrid).
- **Commit:** FastMACE `fastmace/3-T09` at c612a58 plus the driver-id key fix (the tool's own
  `fastmace_commit` field is empty: WSL git cannot read a Windows worktree's `.git` file). Baseline:
  none (no research-engine counterpart).
- **Protocol:** `tools/bench/mc_cost.py`. In one process, MD steps, energy-only evaluations and
  captured swap attempts alternate in timed blocks, rotating and mirrored. 12 rounds, blocks of 100
  (NaCl) or 50 (NbCl5). While timing, the attempts' decisions are forced to reject, so the same
  kernels run and the state stays. Ratios are per round, median shown. Then one real block of 50
  swaps per precision (Metropolis at 1500 K; none accepted) gives the block's device time.
- **Machine:** other workers' CPU suites ran beside the NaCl run: its MD blocks spread by 17 %. The
  NbCl5 blocks spread by 2–12 %.
- **Results:** `results/3-T09-rtx5060ti-20261022/{NaCl,NbCl5}/metadata.json`.

new species, so the MD plan is the MC plan). Two real blocks of 20 attempts per precision, the first

| NaCl (Na ↔ K; Na Cl K) | 11, 35.9 ms, 1.80 ms (98.7 ms) | 0, 661 ms, 33.0 ms (828 ms) |

With μ = 0, Na → K (−2 eV per move) and Nb → Ta (−11 eV) go downhill in MH-1's absolute energies, so
the float32 blocks accept most moves. The float64 rows ran second **with the same seed**: they
re-proposed the atoms the float32 blocks had just changed, as reverse moves (K → Na, Ta → Nb), which go
uphill, so none were accepted. A probe on the GPU checked this: run in the other order, float64 accepts
6 of 6 and float32 then 0 of 6. The trial energy of the captured path equals a direct evaluation of the
same trial state (−1489.66660624930 eV in float64). The tool now gives each precision its own seed.

The swap attempt costs the energy-only evaluation plus about 0.55–0.6 ms: the Philox draws (about
150 small elementwise kernels per draw in plain PyTorch), the selection, and the two refreshes of
the species-derived buffers. Float64 MC energies cost about 12 MD steps per attempt on this GPU.

## Open findings

1. **Attempt overhead.** About 0.55 ms per attempt on top of the energy, which is 19 % of a NaCl MD
   step. Most of it is kernel launches of the plain-PyTorch Philox. A fused Philox kernel (Triton,
   with this module as the CPU reference) would remove most of it. That is a follow-up, not needed for
   correctness.
2. **Float64 MC energies** cost 11.8 (NaCl) to 12.7 (NbCl5) MD steps per swap attempt. With the D3
   protocol's 50 attempts per 100 MD steps, a float64 run would be about 7× slower than MD alone. T11's
   ΔE gate decides whether float32 suffices.
3. **`state.total_charge`** is `None` after construction even for models that take a total charge.
   T09 records a changed total there, and `resume_engine` restores it. A planner that filled it at
   construction would make a fresh construction and a restart agree without that record. That needs a
   decision on old restart files.
4. **The first block's device time includes the attempts' captures** (169 ms for 7 attempts on the tiny
   model). T15's cost line should skip the first block, or report it apart.
