# 3-T09: MC driver, swaps and transmutations

Plan task 3-T09 (`plans/phase-3.md`), 22 October 2026. Spec: §10.4 (all but displacements), §8.5
"Random numbers per outcome", §7.6 (MC blocks), §12.2 "Mutation events", "MC exact enumeration".

## What exists now

- **`fastmace/mc/streams.py`.** Counter-based Philox4x32-10 streams (Random123's known answers), numbers by
  (seed, offset), the fixed counts per attempt, the swap and transmutation decoding and `BlockStreams`
  (the device side of a block). The layout is in `docs/design/contracts-3.md`, "MC streams and the driver".
- **`fastmace/mc/moves.py`.** `Attempt`: propose → apply → refresh → energy → accept mask → restore as
  fixed-shape device operations, one energy stage per edge size (`MCEvaluator.stage`), so the driver
  captures it with `GraphEngine.captured`. `metropolis` (`ln u < −(ΔE − Δμ)/kT`) and `charge_inputs`.
- **`fastmace/mc/momenta.py`.** `exchange`, `rescale`, `redraw` (per move) and `redraw_all` (per block,
  zero total momentum).
- **`fastmace/mc/driver.py`.** `MCDriver(moves, *, seed, config=None)`:
  - `attach(engine, *, evaluator=None)`, `due(step)`, `run_block(engine, step)`, `last_block`, `counters`,
    `offsets`, `state_dict`/`load_state_dict` (the restart's `mc` record, also put into `state.mc`), `info`;
  - one host read per block: counters, attempt indices and the overflow word, with the block's device
    time (CUDA events);
  - the first capture of an attempt runs without effect: the capture's warm-ups restore the engine's
    registered state but not the driver's tensors, so the driver snapshots both around it;
  - after a block with an acceptance: `redraw_all` where chosen, the mass refresh, the total charge as a
    host value, `canonicalise(reevaluate=True)`, then the force-derived caches (T03's resume).
- **`planner.minimal.check_mc_moves`.** Rule 2, the `fixcm` refusals of `rescale` and `redraw`, and the
  charge/spin parity (only for models or heads with charge or spin inputs; otherwise the decision
  "parity: not applicable").
- **`io.restart.resume_engine`.** Restores a recorded total charge through `set_total_charge` (3-T08's open
  point).
- **`tools/bench/mc_cost.py`.** The swap-attempt rows (second editor after T07).

## Deviations

- **`MinimalPlanner._check_atoms`** required the structure's species to *equal* the plan's superset, so a
  `Simulation` with a superset species absent at setup (NaCl with superset Na, Cl, K) could not be
  built, although T02's rule 8 plans it. It now requires the species to be *within* the superset. No test
  pinned the equality.
- **`build_attempt`** takes a keyword-only `fixcm` (the default treatment of a transmutation depends on
  it); `MCDriver.attach` takes a keyword-only `evaluator`. `test_contracts_3.py` pins both.
- **`state.total_charge`** stays the host record of a total-charge change, as T03's `set_charges` keeps it:
  the planner leaves it `None` at construction, also for charged models. After a transmutation that moves
  the total charge it holds the new value, so the restart records it.
