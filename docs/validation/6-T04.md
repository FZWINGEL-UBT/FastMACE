## Selection logic (CPU)

`tests/cpu/test_oeq_prebuilt.py` (16 tests, mocked): a matching manifest is used; not installed, torch/OEq/
CPython mismatch (each named in the reason), missing library or manifest, other platform and
`FASTMACE_OEQ_PREBUILT=0` fall back; `=1` refuses to build; the loader wrapper serves only `libtorch_tp_jit`
and is idempotent; `prepare_in_process` builds nothing with the wheel and records `prebuilt_skipped` without
it; `doctor` reports both paths and only then needs g++/ninja. With `test_oeq_runtime.py` and
`test_cli_doctor.py`: 96 passed (`fastmace_cpu`). Full CPU suite and `ruff check fastmace tests`: see the report.
