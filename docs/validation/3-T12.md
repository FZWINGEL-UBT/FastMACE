## Design and thresholds, fixed in advance (prestart report, conductor's answers)

**Enumeration** (`tests/_support/mc_reference.py`):
- **Configurations:**
  - swaps: NaCl's conventional cell, 8 sites, 4 Na + 4 Cl, 70 configurations;
  - transmutations: NaCl 2×1×1, the 8 cation sites Na or K, Cl fixed, 256 configurations.
- **Reference:** every configuration is evaluated with the float64 reference (MACE's calculator, the same
  weights).
- **Temperature and Δμ:** kT = (energy span) / 3, so the weights span e³. Δμ_K is the least-squares slope of E
  against the K count.
- **The exact chain:** the transition matrix with the driver's proposals. Its stationary vector is the
  Boltzmann weights to 1e-14.
- **Thinning:** k is the smallest number with λ2^k ≤ 0.01.
- **Sample size:** at least 10 expected samples per bin, and power ≥ 0.999 for each power check, from the
  noncentral χ².

**χ² test:**
- pass at p ≥ 1e-3;
- the same counts must give p ≤ 1e-6 against uniform populations and against 1.5 β;
- fixed seeds 20261022 (swap) and 20261023 (transmutation).

**Exact replay:**
- Every decision of `MCDriver.run_block` is predicted from the streams (`fastmace.mc.streams`, the documented
  layout) and the reference energies.
- The driver's species after each block, its acceptances and its ΔE must match.
- Near-ties, |ln u + ΔX/kT| below the margin, are skipped and counted:
  - CPU: margin 1e-6 kT, at most one block with a tie;
  - GPU: margin 0.02 kT, twice the move-energy gate, with at least half the blocks compared.
- ΔE per block must lie within 1e-8 eV (CPU) or 0.01 kT (GPU) of the reference.

**Hybrid MD/MC:**
- Langevin at 1 fs, with an MC block of 10 attempts every 10 steps.
- Kinetic temperature: the total, per species, and the species split T(second) − T(first), each from batch
  means over 10 batches.
- **Pass:**
  - MC on and off agree within 4 combined standard errors, in the total, per species and in the split;
  - the total agrees with the target within 4σ;
  - the split agrees with 0 within 4σ (equipartition).

  The split and its zero target were added after the first negative controls proved weak (finding 3), and
  every hybrid case was rerun with them.
- The negative control (velocities unchanged at a swap, patched in `fastmace/mc/momenta.py`) must fail the
  same test.
- Zero total momentum under `fixcm`: within 1e-12 relative on the CPU and 1e-5 on the GPU.
- Temperatures count 3N degrees of freedom, the integrator's (ASE's) convention; see the findings.

## Findings

1. **The `fixcm` Langevin is exact only for equal masses** (handed to 3-T22, conductor, 22 October 2026).
   - Measured without any MC, on the CPU with Li/Cs at 600 K and `fixcm` on: total 666 ± 8 K, Li 860 ± 26 K,
     Cs 567 ± 15 K. With `fixcm` off: 592 ± 8 K.
   - Cause: `integrators/langevin.py` (ASE's scheme) removes the centre-of-mass part of the noise and scales
     the rest by √(N/(N−1)), which is a correct projection only for equal masses.
   - Hence the cases with `fixcm` use Na and K, where the bias is below the error.
2. **The `fixcm` temperature convention against `redraw_all`** (open for acceptance item 5, 3-T22).
   - The `fixcm` Langevin holds Σ m v² at 3N kT (ASE's √(N/(N−1)) factor).
   - `redraw_all` draws the correct zero-momentum state, (3N−3) kT.
   - So each accepted block lowers the kinetic energy by (N−1)/N, and the GPU runs sit 3.5σ and 5.5σ below MC
     off at N = 16. On the CPU, with the stronger friction, the shift is −10 K, within the noise.
   - The two GPU cases are a non-strict expected failure (`FIXCM_REDRAW_ALL` in
     `tests/gpu/test_mc_statistics_gpu.py`) until 3-T22's corrected projection; then the marker goes.
3. **The negative control with Na and K is weak.** Frequent swaps with unchanged velocities decouple the speeds
   from the masses, so the total heats little and the species temperatures split.
   - With a mass ratio of 1.7 the split was 3.3σ (CPU) and 2.4σ (GPU).
   - My prestart sizing ("total heating ≥ 10σ") assumed velocities uncorrelated with the masses at every swap,
     which is wrong.
   - Two changes followed, both made after seeing the data:
     - the CPU control uses Li and Cs (mass ratio 19): Cs 2152 ± 78 K against 598 ± 15 K, split 1653 ± 68 K;
     - on the GPU, MH-1 accepts almost no Li ↔ Cs swap (6 of 22 000), so the control keeps Na and K with 100
       attempts per block (the swaps outpace the thermostat). A weaker friction instead (0.005/fs) split the
       species more but correlated the samples, giving 3.9σ;
     - the test gained the species split as an observable, compared with MC off and with 0. Per-species
       temperatures carry the run's common-mode fluctuation; the split does not.
   - Every hybrid case, correct ones included, was rerun under the final rule.
4. **The rule-based temperatures are extreme.** The swap enumeration of MH-1 NaCl runs at 56 448 K (Na/Cl
   antisites span about 15 eV) and the tiny model's transmutation at 62 K; the driver accepts both. At fixed
   positions this is legitimate, and it keeps the decisions far from the float32 noise. A test with a
   chemically meaningful temperature needs a pair with a smaller energy span, which 3-T14's protocol supplies.
