# 4-T12b: PolarMACE under cell changes (4-T07's diverging cell relaxation)

Task: the conductor's follow-up to 4-T07, 23 October 2026. ASE's `LBFGS(FrechetCellFilter(atoms))` on
`fm.calc` with POLAR-1-S on NbCl5 3×3×3 diverged in 4-T07: −10694.843 to −10693.860 eV/atom over 5,000
steps, filter fmax 1,845 eV/Å ([4-T07.md](4-T07.md), `results/4-T07-modal-L4-132a5f8/`). The suspected
cause was the PolarMACE stress with its live, cell-dependent k-set (3b-T08).

## Summary

1. **Refuted: the k-set is not the cause.**
   - Over 300 steps of the same relaxation, the included k-set never changed: 3,163 vectors at every step.
   - At the start, the first rising step and the end, `fm.calc`'s forces equal −dE/dx along the force
     direction to 1e-3. Its stress equals dE/dε along hydrostatic and xx strains to about 0.3 %, while
     the k-set grows from 3,149 to 3,183 vectors over ±2e-3. Steps of a few meV at those crossings (3b-T08) are invisible at this scale.
   - The reused calculator equals a fresh one built at the same configuration: energy within 5e-4 eV,
     forces within 4e-4 eV/Å, stress within 5e-7 eV/Å³. Nothing in FastMACE goes stale with the cell.
2. **The cause is POLAR-1-S's own energy surface on this structure.**
   - At 4-T07's start (the r2SCAN cell ×3, scaled by 1.02, rattled by 0.02 Å), the float64 reference
     (`fastmace.reference`, MACE's calculator with the record's corrections) gives a largest force of
     119.64 eV/Å and an rms of 27.4 eV/Å, with 1,250 of 1,944 atoms above 10 eV/Å. FastMACE's production
     path gives the same: 119.64 eV/Å.
   - MH-1 (r2SCAN head) on the same atoms gives at most 1.7 eV/Å.
   - The stress is 0.031–0.039 eV/Å³ on the diagonal, about 6 GPa of tension, against MH-1's 0.015–0.019.
   - ASE's LBFGS (no line search, maxstep 0.2 Å, H0 = 1/70) overshoots on a surface this stiff. The energy
     rises at the first step. **It fails the same way at fixed cell:** 133 rises in 300 steps, fmax still
     167 eV/Å. 4-T07's fmax of 1,845 eV/Å is reproduced at step 112 of the cell run, a configuration with
     atoms 1.1 Å apart.
3. **Safe default, applied:** the planner refuses PolarMACE with a variable-cell optimiser
   (`cell_filter=FrechetCell`; code `polar-variable-cell-optimizer`, rule 1, graph and reference tiers).
   The reason does not blame the k-set. It says that no PolarMACE cell relaxation is qualified (4-T07's
   reference diverged on the model's own forces) and that the k-space energy steps at the cutoff (3b-T08).
   Fixed-cell PolarMACE relaxations and other families' cell relaxations still plan. ASE's own optimisers on
   `fm.calc` are outside the planner and are not refused.
4. **NPT MD with PolarMACE: no refusal proposed.** See "NPT" below.

## NPT

- Integrators (BAOAB, MTK, NPT Berendsen) use the analytic stress. On this evidence it is the derivative of
  the energy wherever the k-set does not change (3b-T08: 1e-9 eV/Å³ in float64; here 0.2–0.3 % in float32
  across crossings).
- Where a k-vector crosses the cutoff, the energy steps and the stress does not see it. 3b-T08 measured
  4.75 meV per crossing pair on 1,944 atoms (2.4 µeV/atom) for a Bragg pair, and nothing measurable for the
  non-Bragg crossings of the ideal cell. Volume fluctuations of 1 % cross about one Bragg pair.
- So NPT sampling, forces and stress are consistent. Only quantities that compare energies across cells
  see the steps, and only by µeV/atom per crossing: the extended-energy drift of MTK, or a volume MC move.
- **Proposal: keep NPT with PolarMACE allowed, with the existing "experimental: not yet qualified" line.**
  Record the extended-energy drift in any PolarMACE MTK qualification. A refusal would remove a working
  path on a µeV/atom effect.
- Separate from the k-set: POLAR-1-S's forces on NbCl5 are about 70 times MH-1's at the same structure.
  Whether POLAR-1-S is fit for NbCl5 at all is a model question for the owner, not a FastMACE defect.

| (b) smooth k-space cutoff | a taper on the Gaussian weights near `kspace_cutoff` in graph_longrange | L, and upstream's choice: it changes the model (an optional-physics entry, non-stock results, a reference with the same taper) | high: a different model; needs upstream or a retraining-free argument that the taper is negligible | none |

**Recommendation: (c) now.** The trigger was the model's own surface, which neither (a) nor (b) changes.
Take (a) if a PolarMACE NPT qualification needs the extended energy conserved to better than the steps.
Lifting the refusal needs a converged reference cell relaxation on a structure where POLAR-1-S is sane
(NaCl is the natural first case).
