# 4-T03: Nose-Hoover chains and MTK NPT

Plan task 4-T03 (`plans/phase-4.md`): ports of ASE 3.27's `NoseHooverChainNVT`,
`IsotropicMTKNPT` and `MTKNPT` (`ase/md/nose_hoover_chain.py`) as graph-capturable
integrators on `SimulationState`, built on 4-T01's scheme registry (draft `bb7917e`).

## What was built

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `fastmace/integrators/nose_hoover.py` | `NoseHooverChain` (one chain on device scalars, shared by the thermostat and both barostats) and `NoseHooverChainNVT` |
| `fastmace/integrators/mtk.py` | `MTK`: `cell_mode="hydrostatic"` is `IsotropicMTKNPT`, `"full"` is `MTKNPT`; `exp_and_exprel` |
| `fastmace/integrators/schemes/nose_hoover.py` | the `nhc` and `mtk` records: ensembles, cell modes, `FixAtoms` refusals, state keys, buffer policies |

- **The split.** ASE's step is cut after the drifts, where the force field evaluates forces and
  stress: `first_half` holds the barostat chain, thermostat chain, cell kick, atom kick and drifts;
  `second_half` the kicks and the chains. The first cell kick uses the previous force call's stress
  (ASE's calculator cache) plus the ideal-gas term of the current momenta, computed on the device.
- **No `eigh`.** `MTKNPT` diagonalises the symmetric `p_g` three times per step; every use is exp or
  exprel of the matrix. `exp_and_exprel` takes both from one scaling-and-squaring Taylor series of
  the 6 × 6 block matrix `[[A, I], [0, 0]]`: no LAPACK, capture-safe, no eigenvector ambiguity at the
  degenerate `p_g = 0` of the start. It matches SciPy to 1e-14 relative.
- **Targets of the step in flight.** `first_half` copies the filled kT and pressure into `kT_step`
  and `pressure_step`, which `second_half` reads, so a pipelined stage B keeps its step's targets.
- **Masses.** Q, R and W come from the construction temperature (conductor's choice 4) and are
  restart state (`mass_temperature_K`); a schedule moves only the target kT.
- **D2** (conductor's answer, 23 October 2026). `N_f = degrees_of_freedom(state)` replaces ASE's 3N
  in every coupling term: the thermostat mass, force and energy, the drag `1 + d/N_f`, the kinetic
  term of the cell force, W, and the full cell's trace term. Under the `fixcm` default, N_f is
  3N − 3: the total momentum is removed at the start and projected out after every step. With
  `dof="ase"` and `fixcm=False`, the arithmetic is ASE's.
- **`FixAtoms`** is refused with ASE's reason (D1(a)).

## ASE 3.27's inconsistencies, ported as they are

- `IsotropicMTKBarostat` gives the first barostat-chain mass 9 kT pdamp² (nine cell degrees of
  freedom), but its chain force targets 1 kT and its energy counts 1 kT per link.
- `MTKBarostat` counts 9 cell degrees of freedom however the cell is constrained (its own TODO;
  ASE 3.29 adds `mask` and `cell_dof`).
- The conserved energies use the target kT, which ASE never changes; with a schedule they are not
  conserved.

## Tests

Commits: FastMACE `fastmace/4-T03` at `f4688c2` (on 4-T01 draft `bb7917e`). No research-engine
baseline: the reference is ASE.

- **CPU:** `tests/cpu/test_nose_hoover_mtk.py`, 36 tests.
  - Against ASE on ASE's Lennard-Jones (108 argon atoms, 2 fs, tdamp 50 fs, pdamp 200 fs, 0.3 GPa):
    positions, velocities and cell within 1e-9 Å after 300 steps; chain variables within 1e-8
    relative; conserved energy within 1e-8 eV.
  - The same with longer chains and loops, a temperature schedule and a pressure change.
  - D2 counts and momentum; extended-energy conservation; fixed addresses and buffer policies.
  - Targets of the step in flight; `state_dict` round trip; restart interrupted against
    uninterrupted, bitwise (with `fixcm` and without); refusals.
  - Command (`fastmace_cpu`: torch 2.14.1+cpu, ASE 3.29.0; also passes in `mace_cueq_latest` with
    ASE 3.27.0):
    `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES='' OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 python -m pytest -n 2 tests/cpu/test_nose_hoover_mtk.py`.
    Result: 36 passed.
- **GPU:** `tests/gpu/test_nose_hoover_mtk_gpu.py`, run with `test_integrators_gpu.py`; 14 passed.
  - Machine and environment: local PC, RTX 5060 Ti, `mace_cueq_latest` (torch 2.14.1+cu130,
    ASE 3.27.0), float64.
  - Captured stages A and B replay the eager steps bitwise, with targets filled between replays, for
    NHC and both MTK cell modes, with and without `fixcm`.
  - The CUDA arithmetic against ASE: 300 steps within 1e-9 Å.

| --- | --- |

is +12 % (NHC) and +28–40 % (MTK). A fused Triton chain stage was proposed to the conductor.
