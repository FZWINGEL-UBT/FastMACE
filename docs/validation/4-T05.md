# 4-T05: GPU LBFGS and the optimiser driver

Plan task 4-T05 ([plans/phase-4.md](../../plans/phase-4.md)); SPEC §9.3 rules 10 and 11, §10.3.
23 October 2026.

## What it is

- **`fastmace/optimize/lbfgs.py`, `DeviceLBFGS`:** ASE 3.27's `LBFGS` on the device, float64, over
  4-T01's `DOFView`. It reproduces ASE's history ring (pops at `iteration > memory`),
  `H0 = 1/alpha`, `determine_step` (the whole step scaled when the longest row reaches `maxstep`),
  `damping`, and `maxstep > 1 A` refused.
  - The two-loop recursion is two unit-triangular solves and six mat-vecs over the ring. A
    captured loop over `memory = 100` pairs would be about 600 kernels per step.
  - When `converged` is set, `propose` leaves the positions and the iteration count bitwise
    unchanged.
- **`fastmace/optimize/driver.py`:**
  - `OptimizerStepper` puts the optimiser in the integrator's place in `GraphEngine`:
    `propose` in stage A, `dof.refresh` and `update` after the force call.
  - `relax(engine, fmax, steps)` is ASE's `Optimizer.run`. A converged start takes no step;
    otherwise it runs at most `steps` steps with one force call each.
- **`engine/graph.py`:** steps are checked only (pipelined and speculative are refused). The
  optimiser's `status` (converged, max force, energy) travels in the host transfer the
  neighbour flags already use, so there is still one sync per step. A step that finds
  `converged` set stops before the force call and is not counted.
- **Planner:**
  - `Plan.optimizer`, and `MinimalPlanner.plan(..., optimizer=)`.
  - Rule 1 is checked against the optimiser's record (`FixAtoms` accepted at fixed cell, D1).
  - Pipelined and speculative are refused.
  - Rule 10: a cell filter turns on the stress path.
  - Rule 11: the skin comes from `maxstep` (below).
  - `fixed.build_stepper` builds the optimiser on the graph, compiled, eager and reference
    tiers, and the fallback re-plans with it.
- **`Simulation`:**
  - It takes exactly one of `integrator=` and `optimizer=`.
  - `sim.relax(fmax=, steps=)` returns the relaxed `Atoms` and a summary. The summary has
    `quench.relax`'s keys plus the per-step energies and fmax, and the rebuild and re-capture
    counts.
  - `run()`, restart writes and `output=` are refused for a relaxation (choice 5). So is a plan
    that would run in a child process.
- **`Simulation.update(cell=)` at fixed cell, for 4-T09:** the cell goes in place into
  `state.cell`, the skin list, the D3 list, MACE's cell inputs and the follower copies. Then
  the update's canonical point rebuilds the lists. Captured graphs stay unless a capacity
  grows or the padded edge size changes.

## CPU tests

Command, from the worktree, at `-n 2`:

```bash
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES='' OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=$PWD ~/environments/fastmace_cpu/bin/python -m pytest -n 2 tests/cpu/test_optimize_lbfgs.py tests/cpu/test_relax_simulation.py tests/cpu/test_update_cell.py
```

- **`test_optimize_lbfgs.py`, Lennard-Jones argon** (108 atoms rattled by 0.15 A), against ASE's
  `LBFGS` on ASE's `LennardJones`:
  - positions within 1e-12 A after every step for 50 steps, eager and on the replay path;
  - the ring wrapping (`memory=3`, 40 steps);
  - `maxstep` scaling with damping;
  - `FixAtoms` (fixed atoms bitwise in place);
  - the same step count to fmax 0.01, a converged start (0 steps, 1 force call), the step
    limit, and a second `run` with a lower fmax;
  - the matrix two-loop against ASE's loops on a random wrapped history (rtol 1e-13);
  - the registry and unregistered-mutation checks.
- **`test_relax_simulation.py`, tiny MH-1-like model on NaCl:**
  - `Simulation(optimizer=)` against ASE's `LBFGS` on MACE's own calculator for the same
    checkpoint, one step at a time, within 1e-12 A for 12 steps, with and without `FixAtoms`;
  - the plan's decisions and rule 11's skin;
  - one engine relaxing a second frame (`update(cell=, positions=)`) bitwise equal to a fresh
    relaxation;
  - every refusal.
- **Why 12 steps, not 50, on the tiny model:** its random landscape is chaotic for LBFGS.
  - ASE's own LBFGS, given forces perturbed by 1e-15 eV/A, drifts 2e-13 A by step 10, 1e-11 A
    by step 20 and 4e-5 A by step 50.
  - It does not reach fmax 0.01 in 1,000 steps.
  - So the plan's 50-step and step-count criteria run on Lennard-Jones, where they hold at
    1e-12 A.
- **`test_update_cell.py`:** MD with a new cell and positions equals a fresh `Simulation`
  (`events.check_against` and three MD steps, bitwise); round trip; refusals.
- **Last run:** targeted files of the change, 708 passed, 119 skipped. They cover the planner,
  Simulation, engine, events, fallback, D3, neighbour lists, contracts and layering.
- **ASE versions:** the CPU environment has ASE 3.29. Its LBFGS is ASE 3.27's algorithm, moved
  to `ase/_4/optimize`; both were read.

## GPU

Machine: local PC, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti, torch 2.14.1+cu130, ASE 3.27.0.
`mace_cueq_latest` for the tests, `mace_cueq_oeq` (hybrid, in-process OEq runtime) for the
frames. Allocator `expandable_segments:True`. FastMACE 2b27876; the run's JSON says `None`
because the tool's `git rev-parse` fails in a WSL worktree. No research-engine baseline was used.

**`tests/gpu/test_lbfgs_gpu.py`: 4 passed.** These run in process on the GPU's
deterministic Lennard-Jones argon.
- The captured relaxation equals the eager one bitwise, step for step, to the same halting
  step, with `memory` 100 and 4. This covers the triangular solves, the ring updates and the
  halt inside the captured graphs.
- It agrees with ASE's LBFGS within 1e-6 A and 2 steps.
- `reset_cell` drops no graph, and the AC and C stages are not re-captured.

**Frames** (`tools/bench/optimizer_frames.py`, results in
[results/4-T05-rtx5060ti-20261023/](../../results/4-T05-rtx5060ti-20261023/)):
- **Set-up:** MH-1 `matpes_r2scan`, hybrid float32. Frames 0, 10 and 20 of the research
  engine's `captured.traj`:
  - NaCl 3x3x3 (216 atoms) from `anneal-NaCl-rtx5060ti-20261010`;
  - NbCl5 (1,944 atoms) from `anneal-production-rtx5060ti-20261010`.
- **Optimiser:** LBFGS with ASE's defaults, fmax 0.01 eV/A, at most 5,000 steps.
- **ASE side:** ASE's LBFGS on `fm.calc` (compiled tier).
- **GPU side:** `Simulation(optimizer=fm.optimize.LBFGS())`, one engine per mode and skin
  relaxing the three frames in turn through `update(cell=, positions=)`.
- **Run:** one process per side, one block each. Timings are recorded, not A/B: cross-process
  noise is about 3 %.

inside D3's scale (0.025 meV/atom, 0.1 A).

In each rebuilds-and-re-captures cell, the first pair is frame 10's and the second frame 20's.

- **Mode:** graph is 2.8–3.9x faster than compiled on NaCl, where per-step overhead matters,
  and 1.1x faster on NbCl5, where the force call dominates. Rule 9's default, the graph tier,
  stands for relaxations.
- **Skin:**
  - 0.3 A rebuilds 2–4x more often than 0.6 A. On NaCl frame 0 (with the captures) it also
    re-captured 4 times against 1, and took 6.2 against 4.4 ms/step.
  - 1.0 A halves the rebuilds again but is no faster.
  - **Rule 11 for optimisers:** skin = max(0.3 A, 3 x maxstep), which is 0.6 A at ASE's
    default maxstep (`neighbors.adaptive.optimizer_skin`). It is general: at least 1.5 full
    steps between rebuilds in a relaxation's first, largest steps. No per-structure table.
- **GPU against ASE (recorded, not acceptance; first frames excluded):**
  - NaCl: 4.1 against 17.7 ms/step, 4.3x.
  - NbCl5: 21.0 against 29.1 ms/step, 1.4x.
  - Whole frames: NaCl frame 10, 0.23 against 0.99 s; NbCl5 frame 10, 6.6 against 9.2 s.
