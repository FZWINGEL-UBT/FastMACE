## What it is

- **`fastmace/optimize/cell.py`, `FrechetCellFilter`:** ASE 3.27's `FrechetCellFilter` on the
  device. It is a `DOFView` with (N + 3) × 3 rows, registered as the cell filter `"frechet"`, so
  `DeviceLBFGS` and `FIRE` run on it unchanged.
  - Options: `scalar_pressure`, `hydrostatic_strain`, `constant_volume`, `mask` (Voigt or 3 × 3)
    and `exp_cell_factor` (default N), as in ASE.
  - The gradient is ASE's `get_forces`: `f @ F` on the atoms, and the Fréchet derivative of
    `expm` against the virial on the cell rows, divided by `exp_cell_factor`. The energy is
    E + pV.
- **Matrix functions on the device,** with no host syncs and no LAPACK, so a step stays
  capturable:
  - `expm_3x3` from `integrators/baoab.py`, now for any square size. 3 × 3 results are bitwise
    unchanged (tested against the old code);
  - `logm_3x3` by inverse scaling and squaring: 4 square roots (product-form Denman–Beavers),
    then an atanh series. It is needed only with a `mask`;
  - ASE's nine `expm_frechet` calls as one 6 × 6 exponential. The adjoint of `L_exp(A, ·)` is
    `L_exp(Aᵀ, ·)`.
- **The ln N offset.** ASE's `FrechetCellFilter` takes the log of `cell_factor · F` with
  `cell_factor = N` (inherited from `UnitCellFilter`), so its cell rows carry a constant
  `exp_cell_factor · ln(N) · I`, about 505 on the diagonal for 108 atoms.
  - ASE evaluates `expm(X / ecf) / N` and `logm(N · F)` on matrices of norm about ln N.
    Against 40-digit mpmath, that costs 2e-14 relative in F and 3e-13 absolute in the cell
    rows.
  - FastMACE evaluates the same quantities shifted: `expm(X / ecf − ln(N) I)` and
    `logm(F) + ln(N) I`. They are exact to 1e-17, and the shifted matrix stays inside
    `expm_3x3`'s range.
- **Range guard.** The fixed iteration counts are exact for |log F| ≤ 0.5 per element (strains
  up to about 65 %). A step outside that range is not applied: `cell_ok` drops, `max_force`
  becomes NaN, and `driver.relax` raises `FloatingPointError` with the filter's reason. The
  check costs nothing extra, because the status read already happens every step.
- **Refusals:** `FixAtoms` with a cell filter (owner decision D1) at the plan, from the scheme
  record; non-periodic cells.
- **Driver:**
  - `OptimizerStepper.start` refreshes the DOF view, so `x` and the gradient are current after
    the first force call and after every re-evaluation.
  - `relax`'s summary gains `cell_A`, `volume_A3`, `stress_GPa` and `scalar_pressure_GPa` for a
    variable cell. With a pressure, the summary's energies are ASE's filter energies, E + pV.
- **Engine and planner:** no change needed. 4-T05's rule 10 already turns on stress and the
  variable-cell engine path for a cell-filtered optimiser: `set_cell`, the deformation-aware
  neighbour test, D3's rebuild on strain and PolarMACE's k-vector check.

### Deviation: LBFGS step-for-step limit 1e-10 Å, not 1e-12

The plan asks for positions within 1e-12 Å at every step for 50 steps.
- **FIRE** holds 1e-12 against `ExactCellRows`. This is ASE's filter with its cell rows
  evaluated in the shifted form. Everything else is ASE's arithmetic, including `get_forces`.
- **LBFGS** cannot hold 1e-12 against any reference that rounds differently. The cell rows of
  `x` sit at |x| ≈ 505 (ASE's definition), where one ulp is 6e-14, while LBFGS's history
  differences `s` are about 1e-4.
  - **Measured on ASE itself:** `ExactCellRows` with ±1 ulp of noise in its cell rows drifts 5e-12
    to 2.5e-11 Å from the noise-free run over 50 steps, on the five filter variants.
  - FastMACE against it: 4e-12 to 4e-11 Å.
  - Stock ASE against itself, with one position moved by 1.6e-14 Å, drifts 4e-12 Å by step 2
    and 9e-11 Å by step 50.
  - So LBFGS is held at 1e-10 Å per step, a few times ASE's own one-ulp drift. The step count
    to fmax is still ASE's exactly.

The second container ran the GPU side of D3 against the first container's ASE side: same

- **NbCl5 with D3** (`dispersion="d3bj"` on both sides, LBFGS):

    rebuilds, 4 re-captures);

- **FIRE:** only the ASE side ran.
  - NaCl converged in 43 steps (1 switch flip).
  - NbCl5 did **not** converge in 5,000 steps (447 switch flips). ASE's own FIRE with the cell
    filter makes no progress here at fmax 0.01, so this crystal has no FIRE reference.

- **PolarMACE (POLAR-1-S):** not run; the Modal share was used up. The k-space cutoff steps
  (3b-T08) remain a known limitation for variable-cell PolarMACE relaxations: the energy is not
  smooth in the cell, so LBFGS can stall short of fmax. Nothing in the code is specific to
  PolarMACE: the engine's k-vector check and redo run for any variable-cell plan.
