- **Steps and switch flips.** Every frame took ASE's step count and the same number of FIRE
  restarts (the steps that took ASE's `v = 0`, `dt *= fdec` branch: the GPU's `restarts` counter,
  ASE's steps where `dt` fell). The float32 risk of the prestart (a `v·f > 0` decision flipping on
  rounding and the paths parting) did not show on these six frames.
- **Minima.** |ΔE| ≤ 3.1e-5 meV/atom and every atom within 5.4e-5 Å of ASE's minimum: the
  float32 force differences between the graph and compiled tiers, not the optimiser.
- **Step time** (recorded only; not an alternating A/B on a quiet machine): the captured GPU
  relaxation took 3 to 5 ms per step on NaCl against 17 to 22 ms for ASE on `fm.calc`, and 21 ms
  against 28 ms on NbCl5, where the force call dominates.
